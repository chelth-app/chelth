# CHELTH BRAND GUIDELINES

Version: B3.1
Status: LOCKED FOUNDATION

## 1. Brand Core

**Brand:** Chelth  
**Descriptor:** Healthcare Workforce Operations  
**Primary line:** Healthcare workforce operations that keep every shift moving.  
**Commercial close:** Fill faster. Coordinate better. Stay in control.

Chelth is a healthcare workforce operations platform for staffing agencies, healthcare facilities, and healthcare professionals.

The brand should feel:

- reliable
- calm
- premium
- operational
- healthcare-native
- trusted
- human
- precise

Chelth should not feel:

- AI-first
- futuristic
- overly technical
- like a generic SaaS startup
- like a recruitment job board
- like a consumer wellness app
- like a hospital management system

## 2. Locked Logo Direction

The approved and locked Chelth identity is the **Shift Mark**.

No future implementation may:

- redraw the Shift Mark
- recreate it with CSS
- recreate it with text
- approximate it with another icon
- alter its internal gaps
- change its proportions
- rotate or skew it
- apply new effects
- generate new logo concepts without explicit approval

All Chelth product and marketing work must use the canonical brand assets in:

`/public/brand/chelth/`

### Canonical logo usage

**Primary logo**
`/brand/chelth/logo-primary.svg`

Use on:

- website headers
- marketing pages
- proposals
- presentations
- sales collateral
- email layouts

**Shift Mark only**
`/brand/chelth/logo-mark.svg`

Use on:

- app shell
- mobile header
- compact navigation
- favicon contexts
- app icon contexts
- loading or compact brand states

**Reverse logo**
`/brand/chelth/logo-reverse.svg`

Use on:

- Chelth Navy
- Chelth Deep Teal
- other approved dark brand surfaces

**Monochrome logo**
`/brand/chelth/logo-monochrome.svg`

Use when:

- one-color printing is required
- neutral documents require a non-color treatment
- specialty production does not permit full color

### Logo clear space

Maintain clear space around the logo equal to at least the approximate height of the Shift Mark's internal opening.

Do not place:

- text
- dividers
- buttons
- photos
- borders
- decorative lines

inside the clear-space area.

### Minimum display sizes

Primary horizontal logo:

- Digital: minimum 140 px wide
- Print: minimum 32 mm wide

Shift Mark:

- Digital: minimum 20 px
- Print: minimum 6 mm

At extremely small sizes, always use the dedicated favicon/app assets rather than shrinking the full primary logo.

## 3. Color System

### Primary brand colors

**Chelth Deep Teal**  
`#126B67`

Primary brand recognition color.

Use for:

- primary CTA
- active states
- key brand accents
- selected navigation
- important graphical elements

**Chelth Teal Dark**  
`#0D514F`

Use for:

- CTA hover states
- dark brand surfaces
- stronger contrast applications

**Chelth Navy**  
`#193348`

Authority color.

Use for:

- headlines
- navigation
- major typography
- dark brand sections
- premium contrast

### Neutral colors

**Chelth Ink**  
`#17272D`

Primary body copy.

**Chelth Slate**  
`#657780`

Secondary copy and metadata.

**Chelth Cloud**  
`#F3F6F5`

Soft background surface.

**Chelth Off White**  
`#FAFBFA`

Primary application canvas.

**Pure White**  
`#FFFFFF`

Cards, forms, modals, tables and elevated surfaces.

### Supporting colors

**Chelth Soft Mint**  
`#B8DDD5`

Use sparingly for:

- secondary visual cues
- supporting brand graphics
- non-critical highlight areas

**Chelth Mint Mist**  
`#E8F3F0`

Use for:

- subtle selected states
- informational panels
- brand background sections

### Semantic colors

Success: `#28826F`  
Warning: `#B57A31`  
Critical: `#B34D4D`  
Information: `#527B96`

Semantic colors must communicate actual operational meaning and should never be used merely for decoration.

## 4. Color Usage Ratio

Typical Chelth marketing page:

- 55% Off White / Cloud
- 20% White
- 12% Navy / Ink
- 8% Deep Teal
- 5% Mint / semantic accents

Avoid turning entire product screens teal.

## 5. Typography

### Marketing / Display

**Manrope**

Recommended weights:

- 500 Medium
- 600 SemiBold
- 700 Bold

Primary use:

- hero headlines
- section headings
- campaign statements
- marketing callouts

### Product / UI

**Inter**

Recommended weights:

- 400 Regular
- 500 Medium
- 600 SemiBold

Primary use:

- application UI
- tables
- forms
- navigation
- operational text
- metadata
- dashboards

### Typography principle

Marketing can have personality.

Product typography should disappear behind the information.

## 6. Voice

Chelth should sound like an experienced healthcare workforce operations team.

Use:

- direct language
- calm confidence
- operational clarity
- measurable outcomes
- plain terminology

Avoid:

- AI-powered
- intelligent workforce engine
- next-generation
- revolutionary
- optimization intelligence
- smart staffing
- disruptive
- autonomous

Preferred examples:

Instead of:

> AI-powered recommendation

Use:

> Suggested workers

Instead of:

> Workforce optimization opportunity detected

Use:

> 3 shifts still need coverage

Instead of:

> Compliance intelligence

Use:

> Credential status

## 7. Brand Messaging

Primary commercial line:

**Healthcare workforce operations that keep every shift moving.**

Supporting statement:

Chelth brings workforce scheduling, staffing requests, credential readiness, shift coverage, and day-to-day coordination into one clear operational platform.

Commercial close:

**Fill faster. Coordinate better. Stay in control.**

## 8. Shape Language

Chelth uses restrained geometry.

- Small radius: 6 px
- Default radius: 8 px
- Large radius: 12 px
- Marketing feature radius: 16 px maximum

Avoid:

- giant rounded cards
- pill-heavy interfaces
- bubble-like components
- overly playful shapes

## 9. Buttons

Primary:

- Chelth Deep Teal background
- White text
- 8 px radius
- Medium/SemiBold
- Minimum 44 px height

Hover:

- Chelth Teal Dark

Secondary:

- White or transparent background
- Subtle slate border
- Navy text

Tertiary:

- Text link
- Optional subtle arrow
- No oversized decorative iconography

## 10. Cards and Surfaces

Cards should feel operational, not decorative.

Default:

- White background
- 1 px border
- Border: `#DDE5E3`
- Radius: 8–12 px
- Minimal or no shadow

Allowed default shadow:
`0 4px 16px rgba(21, 45, 49, 0.06)`

Avoid floating-card overload.

## 11. Iconography

Use a consistent outlined icon system.

Preferred:

- 1.5–2 px stroke
- rounded endpoints
- simple geometry
- operational meaning

Default icon color:

- Slate
- Navy

Teal:

- active
- selected
- important

Do not use multi-color icons unless there is a semantic reason.

## 12. Photography

Chelth photography should focus on healthcare workforce activity.

Prefer:

- professionals arriving for shifts
- clinicians checking schedules
- staffing coordinators working
- facility environments
- workforce check-in
- ID badges
- uniforms
- movement through clinical environments

Avoid:

- staged handshakes
- stock doctors staring at camera
- exaggerated hospital drama
- generic corporate meetings
- turquoise photo overlays

Treatment:

- natural light
- low-to-moderate saturation
- neutral white balance
- calm, premium composition

## 13. Product UI Philosophy

The Chelth product should answer:

**What needs attention?**

before:

**What are our statistics?**

Prioritize:

- uncovered shifts
- staffing requests
- worker action items
- expiring credentials
- pending confirmations
- today's operational activity

Charts should support operations, not dominate the dashboard.

## 14. Marketing vs Product

### Marketing

May use:

- stronger typography
- photography
- dark navy/teal sections
- visual storytelling
- brand graphic devices

### Product

Should use:

- mostly Off White
- White working surfaces
- restrained teal
- clear hierarchy
- minimal decoration
- dense but readable operational information

## 15. Non-Negotiable Implementation Rule

Chelth must visually sell **control over healthcare workforce operations**, not technology for technology's sake.

Any future implementation that introduces generic AI styling, excessive gradients, glowing interfaces, large decorative cards, or a replacement logo should be treated as brand drift.
