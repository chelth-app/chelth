This is a visual translation of the facility workspace onto the locked
Chelth system. It is presentation only:
- no route, capability, permission, staffing-request, sign-off, auth, RLS,
  schema or migration change;
- no service role.

Locked agency pages, Finance, Auth and Worker My Shifts are untouched.
QA-F2 and QA-F3 were not started. No screenshots were saved to the
repository.

--------------------------------
- /app/organisations/<facility>: Facility Overview. Only the facility
  workspace-staff branch changed. The agency Overview and the agency
  non-staff branch render exactly as before (via a `display: contents`
  wrapper).

  and the Request Details drawer.

  only. The agency AgencyTimesheets and the worker's own list (QA-F2) are

- Sidebar: the facility nav label (section 8).
