2. WHAT WAS IMPLEMENTED
======================================================================
A. Global brand stylesheet
   - src/app/globals.css: @import "../styles/brand.css" layer(base).
   - Why a layer: without it, brand.css's element rules (body, h1–h4) are
     unlayered and would override Tailwind utilities regardless of
     specificity, changing existing components unpredictably. Inside the
     base layer, utilities keep precedence.
   - Verified in the compiled CSS: the brand rules sit in @layer base.
   - brand.css itself is untouched. It is added to .prettierignore
     (together with docs/brand and public/brand) so tooling never rewrites
     locked files.

B. Token wiring: one source of values
   - Every brand colour is exposed to Tailwind as chelth-* via
     "@theme inline", mapped to the --chelth-* variables, for example
     bg-chelth-teal, text-chelth-navy, border-chelth-border.
   - Also exposed: font-display (Manrope), shadow-card and shadow-elevated
     (the only approved shadows), rounded-marketing (16 px).
   - Semantic tokens now resolve to the brand variables:

       Token                          Brand value (was)
       -----------------------------  --------------------------------------
       background                     Off White (was #F8FAFC)
       surface                        White
       surface-muted                  Cloud
       foreground                     Ink (was #0F172A)
       border                         #DDE5E3
       primary / primary-hover        #126B67 / #0D514F
                                      (was #0F766E / #115E59)
       secondary                      Navy #193348 (was blue #1E3A8A)
       secondary-hover                Ink
       accent-soft / its foreground   Soft Mint / Teal Dark
       focus-ring                     Navy
       radius-sm                      6 px (was 4 px); md 8, lg 12 unchanged

   - Legacy brand-* aliases now point at the locked values (none are used
     by components).
   - Accessibility exceptions were kept deliberately (section 5).

C. Typography
   - Added @fontsource-variable/manrope 5.3.0, pinned like the existing
     Inter package. It is imported in src/app/layout.tsx.
   - Both fonts are self-hosted: no third-party font requests, and CSP
     font-src 'self' is unchanged. The E2E CSP smoke test passes.
   - Font-name alias:
       * the packages register "Inter Variable" and "Manrope Variable",
         while brand.css names "Inter" / "Manrope";
       * globals.css prepends the registered names to --chelth-font-ui and
         --chelth-font-display (same families, resolvable);
       * without this, headings would silently fall back to a generic
         sans-serif.
   - Verified in a production build:
       * body is Inter, Ink on Off White;
       * h1–h4 are Manrope, Navy;
       * the primary button is Deep Teal with an 8 px radius;
       * document.fonts reports both fonts loaded.
   - Note: brand.css renders h1–h4 in Manrope in the product too. That
     follows the locked file; see section 6, item P2-7.

D. Metadata
   - Viewport themeColor changed from #0f766e to Deep Teal #126B67.
   - Icons, manifest and titles: unchanged (section 4).

E. Source-of-truth lock for future work
   - CLAUDE.md now includes docs/brand/CLAUDE-CHELTH-BRAND-RULES.md, so
     future Claude sessions load the brand rules automatically. AGENTS.md
     was not edited: it is regenerated by `next dev`.
   - docs/architecture/DESIGN_SYSTEM.md was rewritten for tokens,
     typography and logo usage. It points to the brand documents as the
     authority and records the accessibility exceptions.

6. REMAINING BRAND DRIFT (prioritised; NOT changed in this pass)
======================================================================
P1 — identity and first impression
  1. BrandMark (src/components/shared/brand-mark.tsx) typesets "CHELTH" in
     letter-spaced text. It is used on:
       - the home page;
       - the auth card (sign-in, sign-up, password reset);
       - the app header;
       - the design-system page.
     This violates the locked "never typeset the wordmark" rule. Replace it
     with the logo-primary (and logo-mark in compact headers) once the clean
     exports exist.
  2. APP_NAME = "CHELTH" (all caps). The brand writes "Chelth", and the
     descriptor in title case: "Healthcare Workforce Operations". This
     affects page titles, metadata and email subjects.
  3. Favicon, apple-touch icon and web manifest are absent (blocked,
     section 4).
  4. Email surfaces use pre-brand colours and no logo lockup:
       - notification templates (src/lib/notifications/templates.ts:
         #0f766e, #0f172a, #475569, #f1f5f9);
       - the invitation email (#0f766e, #475569);
       - the Supabase Auth templates (supabase/templates/*.html: text
         wordmark "CHELTH").
     The guidelines specify logo-primary in email layouts. The notification
     banner is 1.6 MB.
  5. Home page:
       - the headline is not the approved line ("Healthcare workforce
         operations that keep every shift moving.");
       - the call-to-action links are hand-rolled at about 40 px high,
         below the 44 px minimum;
       - the page does not use Button.

P2 — components
  6. Status colours use the generic Tailwind palette:
       - danger #B91C1C instead of Critical #B34D4D;
       - success, warning and info soft pairs are blue, green and amber
         tints instead of brand semantic colours;
       - the "info" badge reads as blue, off-brand.
     Define brand-derived soft pairs (AA-checked) and re-point the tokens.
  7. Heading typography in product screens: brand.css puts all h1–h4 in
     Manrope, while the guidelines say product UI typography should be
     Inter. Decide whether product headings stay Manrope (current) or use
     Inter with Manrope reserved for marketing; then adjust the brand
     stylesheet or add a product scope.
  8. Card uses shadow-xs and Dialog uses shadow-lg. Switch to shadow-card /
     shadow-elevated (the approved tokens).
  9. The outline (secondary) button uses the slate input border and ink
     text. Brand secondary is a #DDE5E3 border with Navy text; reconcile
     with the 3:1 control-contrast requirement (section 5).
  10. Disabled and "Saving" buttons render as faded teal (opacity).
      Define a neutral disabled treatment.
  11. App header navigation:
        - no Shift Mark;
        - no active or selected state (brand: teal for selected
          navigation);
        - plain text links.
  12. Organisation section links are bordered pill-like buttons. Consider
      a tab or secondary-navigation pattern with a selected state.

P3 — consistency
  13. The Badge "brand" tone uses Soft Mint as a background. The guidelines
      reserve mint for sparing, supporting use; prefer Mint Mist for
      selected states.
  14. Dense operational lists (shifts, candidates, offers, operations) are
      card lists. The guidelines prefer tables for dense operational
      information.
  15. The error, not-found and global-error pages use the text BrandMark
      and generic copy.

Voice audit: no AI-centric wording ("AI-powered", "intelligent", "smart",
"optimisation") was found in product copy.
