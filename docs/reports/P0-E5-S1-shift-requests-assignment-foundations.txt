======================================================================
1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
======================================================================
Chelth now has its first operational staffing layer:
  - agency-created shifts and facility staffing requests (one entity);
  - worker assignments with explicit acceptance;
  - a single server-side eligibility decision that answers "can this exact
    person be assigned to this exact facility for this exact shift date
    without violating compliance, tenancy, capacity or schedule
    constraints?".

The decision:
  - calls the P0-E4-S1 compliance engine, scoped to the shift's facility and
    discipline, on every local calendar date the shift touches;
  - requires readiness = ready;
  - blocks the same PERSON from overlapping active assignments across all
    agencies, without revealing the other agency;
  - holds headcount under concurrency (row lock + trigger + tests);
  - records every allowed or refused attempt in an append-only decision
    table.

Facilities and workers see only narrow projections. Every item of the
completion gate is met. There are no unresolved HIGH or CRITICAL findings.

Validation results:
  - pgTAP: 618 assertions (190 new).
  - Integration: 57 tests (13 new).
  - E2E: 60 runs across desktop and mobile (10 new).
  - Unit: 152 tests (24 new).

3. SHIFT DOMAIN MODEL
======================================================================
There is one entity, `shifts`: work an agency fulfils for a client facility.
A facility request is a shift with source = facility that starts as
`submitted`. Because there is no second table, a request and its shift
cannot drift apart.

Each shift belongs structurally to the agency, the agency's client facility
record, that facility's relationship, and one of that facility's locations.
All of these are composite foreign keys, so they hold even for the database
owner.

Other fields:
  - Discipline: a foreign key to the disciplines reference data.
  - Headcount: a column (1–100) on one row. Four CNAs means one shift with
    up to four active assignments. The upper bound is a sanity check.
  - No rates or payroll fields.

Documentation: docs/architecture/SHIFT_DOMAIN_MODEL.md.

======================================================================
4. SHIFT TIME MODEL
======================================================================
Storage:
  - start_at and end_at are timestamptz (canonical UTC).
  - timezone is the location's IANA zone. A trigger checks it matches the
    location.

Input and conversion:
  - Commands take wall-clock date and times in the location timezone. The
    server converts them.
  - An end time at or before the start time means the shift is overnight.
  - Equal start and end times mean a 24-hour shift.
  - A local time that doesn't exist (the DST gap) is rejected.
  - An ambiguous local time resolves to the standard-time instant.

Constraints:
  - end_at > start_at.
  - Elapsed duration is at most 25 hours: a 24-hour wall-clock shift across
    a DST fall-back. This is the only cap, and it is documented.

Compliance dating: the engine is evaluated on every local date the shift
touches.

Display always uses the shift's timezone, e.g. "7:00 PM – 7:00 AM (+1 day) EDT".

Documentation: docs/architecture/SHIFT_TIME_MODEL.md.

======================================================================
5. SHIFT LIFECYCLE
======================================================================
Stored states are business-authoritative only:
  - draft → open → completed (completion only after end_at);
  - submitted → open;
  - draft, submitted or open → cancelled, with a controlled reason:
    facility_cancelled, staffing_no_longer_needed, entered_in_error,
    relationship_suspended or other.

Unfilled, partially filled and filled are DERIVED from active assignments vs
headcount. They are never stored.

Enforcement by trigger:
  - Terminal rows are immutable (CHS09).
  - Only legal transitions are allowed.
  - Location, discipline and times are fixed once a shift is open.
  - Headcount can never drop below the number of active assignments.

Cancelling a shift cancels its active assignments with reason
shift_cancelled and notifies the affected workers.

======================================================================
6. FACILITY REQUEST MODEL
======================================================================
Submitting a request:
  - A linked facility user with shift.request submits through ONE explicit
    ACTIVE relationship (authz.has_relationship_capability).
  - The location must belong to that relationship's facility.
  - A facility-sourced row's creator must be a member of the LINKED facility
    organisation. A trigger enforces this, and the pgTAP test runs as DB
    owner.

The agency keeps control: a request stays `submitted` until the agency opens
it. The UI button is "Accept and open request".

Withdrawal: the facility may withdraw only its own request, only while it is
still submitted, and only with reason facility_cancelled. Editing is not
supported in this stage; the facility withdraws and resubmits.

Audit: submission is recorded in both organisations. A
facility_request_submitted hook is sent to the agency, and a
facility_request_opened hook is sent to the facility.

======================================================================
7. ASSIGNMENT DOMAIN MODEL
======================================================================
`shift_assignments` holds agency_worker_id, profile_id and a copy of the
shift period. Composite foreign keys guarantee that:
  - the worker belongs to the shift's agency;
  - the profile is that worker's person;
  - the copied period equals the shift's period;
  - the actor is a member of the same agency.

`period` is a generated column: tstzrange [start, end).

There are no attendance or timesheet fields.

Documentation: docs/architecture/ASSIGNMENT_DOMAIN_MODEL.md.

======================================================================
8. ASSIGNMENT LIFECYCLE
======================================================================
Transitions:
  - assigned → accepted | declined | cancelled
  - accepted → cancelled

Active means assigned or accepted. Active assignments consume headcount and
block the person's time. Declined and cancelled are terminal and consume
neither. A trigger enforces the transitions (CHA09).

Worker actions:
  - The worker explicitly accepts or declines their OWN assignment
    (authz.is_own_active_worker).
  - Acceptance re-checks eligibility live, and requires an open shift that
    has not ended under an active relationship.

Cancellation reasons are controlled: shift_cancelled is set only by the
system; the others are worker_unavailable, compliance_change,
entered_in_error, relationship_suspended and other.

======================================================================
9. COMPLIANCE GATE
======================================================================
internal.assignment_eligibility calls
internal.evaluate_compliance(worker, shift facility, date, shift discipline)
for every local shift date. The P0-E4-S1 engine is the only rule source; no
credential logic is duplicated. The engine covers:
  - agency baseline and facility requirements;
  - sharing, and verification by THIS agency;
  - document trust (clean documents only);
  - expiry on the date, minimum validity and jurisdiction.

The engine gained a discipline scope. The 3-argument form now delegates to
the 4-argument form, so there is still one implementation.

The gate also checks:
  - the worker record and membership are active (WORKER_NOT_ACTIVE);
  - the worker holds the discipline (DISCIPLINE_MISMATCH);
  - capacity (SHIFT_FULL);
  - duplicates (ASSIGNMENT_ALREADY_EXISTS);
  - person-level schedule (WORKER_SCHEDULE_CONFLICT).

Policy: the worker must be READY. action_required (for example
EXPIRING_SOON on the shift date) BLOCKS assignment. The justification is in
ASSIGNMENT_ELIGIBILITY.md: it is the agency's own warning rule, tunable per
requirement.

Refusals return structured codes, compliance reasons, and plain-language
findings. Compliance detail is shown only to callers with compliance.view.

======================================================================
10. COMPLIANCE DECISION / AUDIT MODEL
======================================================================
`assignment_eligibility_decisions` is append-only; UPDATE and DELETE are
refused even for the owner. It stores one row per attempt, allowed or
refused:
  - outcome and block reasons;
  - readiness;
  - compliance reason codes and findings (scope, credential type, reason,
    severity, evaluation date, effective expiry);
  - evaluation dates and engine_version (compliance-engine.p0-e5-s1);
  - actor membership, sequence and timestamp.

It never stores credential numbers, documents, or anything about another
agency. It proves a decision at a point in time and never replaces live
evaluation.

Refusals are RETURNED, not raised, so the decision and audit event commit.
Reading decisions requires assignment.view AND compliance.view; this was
hardened during the security review.

Audit events:
  - Shifts: shift.created, submitted, opened, updated (field names only),
    cancelled, completed, internal_note_added,
    assignments_viewed_by_facility.
  - Assignments: assignment.created, accepted, declined, cancelled,
    rejected_by_compliance, rejected_by_conflict, rejected_by_capacity.

======================================================================
11. SCHEDULE CONFLICT MODEL
======================================================================
Two assignments conflict when both are active and their half-open intervals
[start, end) overlap. Back-to-back shifts (08:00–12:00 and 12:00–16:00) are
allowed; a one-minute overlap conflicts (both tested).

Enforcement has three layers:
  - The RPC checks for conflicts under a per-person advisory lock.
  - A structural backstop: EXCLUDE USING gist (profile_id WITH =, period
    WITH &&) WHERE active. It holds for every writer, including the table
    owner.
  - Acceptance re-checks.

======================================================================
12. MULTI-AGENCY CONFLICT MODEL
======================================================================
Conflicts resolve to the PERSON (profile_id), not the agency worker record.
One person working for Agency A and Agency B cannot hold overlapping active
assignments.

Privacy:
  - Agency B receives only WORKER_SCHEDULE_CONFLICT ("Worker has a
    scheduling conflict").
  - The result, the decision row and the audit metadata contain no Agency A
    id, facility, time or shift id. This is tested in pgTAP (N) and in
    integration.
  - Agency B still cannot read Agency A's assignments.

======================================================================
13. HEADCOUNT CONCURRENCY MODEL
======================================================================
assign_worker_to_shift takes two locks in a fixed order, which prevents
deadlocks:
  1. SELECT … FOR UPDATE on the shift row, which serialises capacity per
     shift;
  2. pg_advisory_xact_lock(hash('chelth.schedule:' || profile)), which
     serialises schedule checks per person.

Backstops:
  - The capacity trigger re-locks the shift and counts (CHS16).
  - A unique partial index enforces one active assignment per
    (shift, worker).
  - The exclusion constraint also prevents duplicate assignments.

Headcount reductions are serialised on the same row lock.

Integration tests:
  - Two schedulers racing for the last slot: exactly one is allowed and the
    other gets SHIFT_FULL.
  - A duplicate race: one is allowed and the other gets
    ASSIGNMENT_ALREADY_EXISTS.

18. STRUCTURAL INTEGRITY
======================================================================
Composite foreign keys:
  - shift → agency_facilities (id, agency)
  - shift → relationships (id, agency, facility)
  - shift → locations (id, facility)
  - shift creator → memberships (id, organisation)
  - assignment → shifts (id, agency, start, end)
  - assignment → agency_workers (id, agency, profile)
  - assignment actor → memberships
  - decision → shift, worker, assignment and actor, each with the agency

Triggers:
  - Facility-request creator must belong to the linked facility.
  - Shift timezone equals the location's timezone.
  - Lifecycle transitions and capacity.
  - Ownership and identity columns are immutable.

Exclusion constraint: one person, no overlapping active assignments.

Every one of these was tested as the DB owner (pgTAP U, M, O, P).

26. SECURITY FINDINGS
======================================================================
Reviewed areas, each with its disposition:
  - Cross-tenant shift IDOR: SHIFT_NOT_FOUND for missing or invisible

    B).
  - Facility relationship bypass: only has_relationship_capability, with
    ACTIVE required for requests and no table path. Tested (C, D).
  - Worker self-assignment: workers can't see shifts, and an agency member
    can't assign their own record. Tested (F).
  - Compliance gate bypass: there is no insert grant; the RPC is the only
    path; the capacity trigger and the constraints hold for the owner.
    Tested (H–K, X).
  - Discipline mismatch: gated, and the engine is discipline-scoped. Tested
    (J).
  - Date and expiry evaluation: every local shift date is evaluated; the
    DST gap is rejected. Tested (I, time tests).
  - Multi-agency conflicts: an exclusion constraint on the person. Tested
    (M).
  - Privacy leak through conflict messages: a generic code only; nothing in
    the result, decision or audit. Tested (N).
  - Concurrent overfill: row lock plus trigger. Tested in integration.
  - Duplicate assignment: unique index plus exclusion. Tested (P).
  - Acceptance spoofing: ASSIGNMENT_NOT_FOUND for anyone but the owner.
    Tested (G).
  - Cancelled-shift mutation: terminal rows are immutable. Tested.
  - Internal-note leakage: separate table, agency-only RLS, never in
    projections or audit. Tested.
  - Assigned-worker overexposure: exact field list, audited reads. Tested
    (S).
  - Platform-admin overreach: no RLS or projection path. Tested (T).
  - Service-role exposure: none used; the bundle scan is clean.

MEDIUM, fixed: decision rows contain compliance findings but were readable
with assignment.view alone. RLS now requires compliance.view as well.

LOW, accepted:
  - The facility's readiness indicator is an aggregate, which the assignment
    justifies.
  - The candidate conflict flag reveals the same single bit as a refusal.

There are no unresolved HIGH or CRITICAL findings.

27. KNOWN LIMITATIONS
======================================================================
  - Candidate evaluation runs the engine per worker; this is fine for
    agencies of normal size. A set-based variant is needed for very large
    rosters.
  - list_agency_shifts and list_facility_shifts are capped at 500 rows, with
    no pagination.
  - The UI "Save details" action re-sends the stored wall-clock times. For a
    shift starting in the ambiguous DST fall-back hour, an edit would be
    refused once the shift is open (CH409) rather than silently shifting it.
  - Facilities can't edit a submitted request; they withdraw and resubmit.
  - No scheduler consumes the outbox or runs scan_assignment_readiness yet.
  - Past-dated shifts can't be created; retroactive entry is a timesheet
    concern.
  - The untracked tests/e2e/credentials-compliance-ci-check.spec.ts, which I
    did not create, near-duplicates credentials-compliance.spec.ts and runs
    those flows twice. I left it in place for the owner to decide.
