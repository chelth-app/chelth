P0-E9-4A.2 — ORGANISATION GATEWAY CLEANUP & BILLING-SAFE MULTI-BUSINESS CREATION
CHELTH — Healthcare Workforce Operations
Date: 2026-10-14
Branch: p0-e8-visual-implementation (uncommitted; not deployed)

Summary
-------
- /app is now an organisation switcher. Only someone with no organisation is
  offered "Create your staffing agency".
- An agency owner can no longer self-create a second agency. The database
  refuses it (CHB01), so a direct RPC call, an old frontend or the console
  cannot bypass it.
- An additional business is a request (Account → Businesses → Add another
  business). Chelth fulfils it in one transaction that creates the agency
  AND its own commercial account. Nothing is inherited.
- Being a member (worker, scheduler…) is not ownership, so those people can
  still start their own first agency.
- customer_accounts is now structurally one-per-agency and cannot share
  provider IDs.

====================================================
AUDIT (§1)
====================================================

- Who could create an agency: any signed-in user with a verified email,
  through public.create_organisation('agency'); facility types were refused
  (platform only).
- Unlimited? Effectively yes: the only limit was a rate limit of 5 per
  identity per day. No ownership check existed, and hiding the UI would not
  have stopped a direct RPC call.
- Owner semantics: the creator gets an active membership + the owner role
  (agency.admin, roles.is_owner_role). Platform-created agencies invite the
  owner instead.
- Commercial account: never created automatically (4A design: "Commercial
  setup required").
- Server-side constraints on the number owned: none.
- Production: the read-only production query was refused (the linked
  account now gets 403 "does not have the necessary privileges", so the
  access token's permissions have changed). The last snapshot (P0-E9-4,
  2026-10-11) had 5 agencies and 5 agency-admin memberships; whether one
  person owns several is UNVERIFIED. Nothing in this phase changes existing
  data, so any existing multi-agency owner is preserved as is (no suspension,
  merge or charge).

====================================================
3–5. OWNERSHIP · FIRST AGENCY · ADDITIONAL BUSINESS
====================================================

Ownership (authoritative, internal.owned_agency_count): an active
membership holding an active OWNER role in a non-archived agency.
Membership without the owner role is not ownership. Facility ownership
(facility.admin in a facility organisation) does not count.
First agency: unchanged self-service (create_organisation) when the caller
owns no agency. A worker, recruiter or scheduler elsewhere may create their
own (tested).
Additional business (owner):
- Account → Businesses: "Add another business" (secondary; collapsed),
  with: "Use this only if you operate a separate staffing business.
  Additional businesses have separate Chelth subscriptions, teams and data.
  Branches and new facilities belong inside your existing agency."
- form: business name, billing contact email, optional note →
  request_additional_business (owners only; CHB02 for non-owners; ≤ 3
  pending; rate limited 5 per day) → "Waiting for Chelth"; the owner may
  withdraw.
- the platform fulfils (platform_fulfil_business_request: trial or active,
  optional plan and trial end, reason) or declines with a reason.

====================================================
6. SERVER-SIDE BYPASS PREVENTION
====================================================

create_organisation (replaced, same signature) raises CHB01
(ADDITIONAL_AGENCY_REQUIRES_COMMERCIAL_SETUP) when the caller owns an
agency, before any write. Verified via pgTAP (as the authenticated role),
the integration test (supabase-js API client) and E2E (the browser
session's API client). The UI is presentation only.

====================================================
7–8. CUSTOMER-ACCOUNT RELATIONSHIP · FUTURE BILLING INVARIANT
====================================================

Reused public.customer_accounts (no new billing table):
- unique organisation_id (one account per organisation, existing);
- NEW organisation_type column (= 'agency') + FK (organisation_id,
  organisation_type) → organisations(id, type): structurally agency-only;
- NEW unique (provider, provider_customer_id) and (provider,
  provider_subscription_id): one provider identity cannot be shared by two
  agencies;
- never attached to profiles.
Invariant: every independent agency = one independent commercial account.
Fulfilment creates the agency and its account in one transaction from
explicit inputs; no plan, trial, seats or status is copied from the
requester's other business (tested: the first agency's account is unchanged
and separate). The approved request is the one-time commercial
authorisation (pattern B): it cannot be fulfilled twice (CH409), and there is
no other path to an additional active agency.
Future provider (P0-E9-4B): request → plan selection → checkout →
successful activation → fulfilment. Server-side only; an abandoned payment
leaves only a request, never a usable agency.
FIRST AGENCY COMMERCIAL ACTIVATION: provider integration / commercial
policy deferred. The pilot keeps self-service first agencies ("Commercial
setup required" until configured). Nothing hard-codes "first agency = free".
ADDITIONAL AGENCY COMMERCIAL ACTIVATION: cannot bypass an independent
commercial account.

====================================================
9. PLATFORM FALLBACK
====================================================

/platform/billing → Additional business requests:
- each request shows the business, the requester (name, email), the agencies
  they already own, the billing contact, the note and the date;
- actions: Create business and account (status, plan, trial end, reason) or
  Decline (reason);
- decided requests show Created (with a link to the new business), Declined
  or Withdrawn;
- the pending count appears under Overview → Needs attention; the events
  appear in Activity.
platform_create_organisation (4A) is unchanged for initial provisioning;
for an existing owner's second business, operators use the request path
(PILOT_OPERATIONS.md §12).
Platform-admin authority is unaffected by the first-agency rule.

====================================================
10–12. NON-OWNER MEMBERSHIP · FACILITIES VS BUSINESS · BRANCHES
====================================================

Non-owner members:
- they see no creation panel on /app (their organisations are listed);
- Account → Businesses offers "Create your staffing agency";
- the RPC allows their first owned agency (tested: worker of two agencies,
  scheduler).
Facilities vs business: the Businesses panel says hospitals, nursing homes
and clinics are Facilities inside the agency workspace, not separate
businesses. Facility organisation creation is unchanged: agency-initiated
connection (4A.1) and platform fallback; none from /app.
Branches (P0-FUTURE, documented only): Agency → branches/business units
(e.g. Lagos, Abuja), under one agency and one commercial account unless
future pricing says otherwise. Extra agencies must never substitute for
branches. Not built.

====================================================
13–14. PRIVACY / RLS · AUDIT
====================================================

additional_business_requests: RLS on, no policies, no grants (direct access
⇒ 42501). Owners read only their own (list_my_additional_business_requests);
the platform reads all through an audited projection. No global business
search, no other organisations, billing contacts, accounts or users are
exposed to tenants. Tenant RLS is unchanged.
Audit:
- additional_business.requested, .created, .declined, .withdrawn;
- platform.customer_account_created (cause additional_business);
- organisation.created (via platform, cause additional_business);
- membership.created.
Metadata: IDs, status, plan key and reason only. Never the email or any
payment data (pgTAP P).
