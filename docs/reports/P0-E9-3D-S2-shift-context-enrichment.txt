1. MIGRATION (local only)
-------------------------
supabase/migrations/20261010100000_shift_context.sql. All changes are
nullable or additive.

agency_facilities, new columns:
- parking_instructions, arrival_instructions: ≤ 500 characters, newlines
  allowed.
- worker_contact_label: 2–80 characters; a role or desk.
- worker_contact_phone: business phone format. The label and phone are set
  together or not at all.
- image_path: bucket-relative; it must sit under its own agency / facility
  prefix.
- image_updated_at.

shifts:
- unit_label: optional, 1–80 characters.

Storage bucket facility-images: private, 2 MB, JPEG / PNG only.
- read: agency facility.view, or a worker with an active assignment there,
  and only for the facility's current image;
- upload / delete: agency facility.manage at AAL2, inside that facility's
  prefix;
- no update path.

Helpers:
- authz.has_active_assignment_at_facility;
- authz.can_read_facility_image;
- authz.can_write_facility_image;
- internal.facility_image_owner.

RPCs:
- update_facility_worker_context (facility.manage, AAL2; the audit lists
  which fields changed, never their values);
- set_facility_image (facility.manage; the image must already exist in
  storage under that facility's prefix; returns the previous path for
  removal; audited);
- set_shift_unit (shift.manage; no-op when unchanged; audited).

list_my_shift_assignments was replaced: same name and argument, with extra
columns.
- New columns: agency_facility_id, local_date (facility-local), unit_label,
  the address (the location's own address when it has one, otherwise the
  facility's), parking / arrival guidance, worker contact, image_path.
- The context is returned ONLY while the assignment is active (assigned /
  accepted on an open shift) — the existing instructions rule.
- Site phone, site e-mail and internal notes are never returned.

Validation:

2. TODAY / UPCOMING / PAST
--------------------------
src/lib/domain/shifts.ts shiftPeriod() (unit-tested in
tests/unit/lib/shift-context.test.ts). It uses the facility's own
calendar, with "now" from the server render:
- today: starts on the facility's current local date, or is in progress
  (a night shift that began yesterday);
- upcoming: a later local date;
- past: earlier and over.
Example: 23:30 in New York on the 10th is "today" there, even though it is
the 11th in UTC.

My Shifts (canonical screen 1):
- The segmented control (nav "Shift period", aria-current).
- Today shows today's shifts as full cards. With none, it shows the "Next
  Shift" card, then up to three "Upcoming Shifts" rows with "View all".
- Upcoming shows every future shift as a card. Past shows compact rows.
- Offers stay at the top of Today and Upcoming.
- Accept / Decline remain on the card while the worker can respond.


3. FACILITY IMAGE
-----------------
Agency: Facility record → new "For workers" panel (requires facility.manage
and AAL2):
- the upload is a one-time signed upload;
- the server downloads and verifies size (≤ 2 MB) and the JPEG / PNG
  signature, then attaches the image. Bytes that fail are removed and
  refused ("not a JPG or PNG");
- replacing an image removes the old object; "Remove photo" clears it.

Worker:
- a short-lived (1 h) signed URL per render, only for facilities with an
  active assignment;
- shown on the card thumbnail and the Shift Details hero;
- the service worker never caches it (cross-origin).

Fallback: the approved Chelth facility artwork (the W1 luminous tile
language). It is also used when an image cannot be shown. No generated or
stock photos.

Accepted risk (S1 D4): facility images are not malware-scanned. They are
staff-uploaded at AAL2, signature-verified, and only ever rendered as
<img>.


4. FACILITY ADDRESS
-------------------
Shift Details shows the street, then "City, Region Postcode", with a pin.
It is the location's own address when one is recorded, otherwise the
facility's. No field is duplicated.


5. DIRECTIONS
-------------
"Get directions" is a destination-only deep link:
- Apple devices: maps.apple.com/?daddr=…;
- everything else: Google Maps dir/?api=1&destination=… (the native app on
  Android when installed, the browser otherwise).

Only the facility address is sent. Asserted: no worker name, unit, contact
or ids in the link.


6. UNIT / DEPARTMENT
--------------------
- The agency shift "Manage shift" form has an optional "Unit / department"
  field (set_shift_unit), shown in the shift Details.
- Workers see it on the card and in Shift Details when present.


7. PARKING / ARRIVAL
--------------------
- Facility defaults: Parking, and Arrival and check-in.
- The existing shift instructions are the per-shift override. On Shift
  Details they are shown together in an "Arrival" card as "Parking",
  "Arrival and check-in" and "For this shift".


8. WORKER-FACING CONTACT
------------------------
- An opt-in role / desk label plus a business phone (S1 D1). No personal
  contact names, consistent with the facility domain invariant.
- Shown as "Facility contact · <label> · <phone>" with a tel: Call button
  (accessible name "Call <label>"), and again under "Need help?".
- The site's business phone and e-mail are NOT shown (asserted).


9. REQUIREMENTS
---------------
- The existing engine is read through getReadiness(worker, facility):
  evaluate_worker_compliance and worker_readiness, which already allow a
  worker their own record.
- Each requirement shows the credential name, "Valid until <date>" or the
  engine's reason, with a chip for issues.
- "Update my credentials" appears when anything needs action.
- Nothing is recomputed on the client.


10. SHIFT DETAILS (canonical screen 2)
--------------------------------------
New route: /app/organisations/<id>/my-shifts/<assignmentId>. It replaces
the drawer, matching the canonical separate screen.

Content, in order:
- back "My Shifts";
- facility hero image (or artwork);
- facility name (h1) and address;
- status chip;
- the date / time block with the length ("(8 hours)");
- facts: Role, Unit, Location, Facility contact with Call;
- Get directions;
- Arrival;
- Requirements;
- Need help? (Call contact, Get directions);
- Accept / Decline when the worker can respond;
- the attendance section.

Only real data is shown:
- a past shift shows its record, not live context (no address, contact,
  guidance or requirements);
- an inactive assignment receives no context from the server.


11. CHECK-IN CONTEXT AND CHECKED-IN STATE (canonical screen 3)
--------------------------------------------------------------
Attendance section, using the unchanged ClockControl / BreakControl logic:
- Not started: "Check In", with context (facility · date · time · role ·
  unit), then the canonical location verification panel. Nothing is
  claimed before the server confirms.
- Clocked in: "Checked in successfully — today at <server time>".
  - A "Current Shift" row (scheduled time, role, unit) with a "Checked In"
    chip.
  - Then Clock out, Start break, and "You can check out when your shift is
    complete."
- On a break: the existing panel and End break.
- There are no pay or earnings figures.


12. TIMESHEET HANDOFF (canonical screen 4)
------------------------------------------
After clock-out: "Worked Time", with a "From check in / out" chip and a
summary of:
- Check In and Check Out (server times, "Recorded" unless under review);
- Total Hours;
- Break (unpaid, attendance-derived);
- Worked Hours.

Then:
- the week, with its real timesheet status;
- "Review and submit timesheet" (when the week can be submitted) or "View
  timesheet";
- "Your timesheet is sent to your agency for review and approval."

Notes are omitted: not supported (no column, weekly submission).

14. SECURITY CONFIRMATION
-------------------------
- No RLS weakened.
- Context is exposed only through the worker's own projection while
  active.
- Images: bucket policies plus server signature checks.
- No private contact is exposed (the site phone / e-mail are withheld; the
  worker contact is explicit and opt-in).
- Directions send the destination only.
- No service role.


15. GAPS
--------
- Facility users' projection does not yet show the unit (not required).
- Offers do not show the address or contact before acceptance (the existing
  narrow offer projection is kept).
- A broken image whose error fires before hydration may show the browser
  placeholder; the server-side signature check makes this unlikely.
