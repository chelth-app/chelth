7. GEOFENCE / LOCATION STATES
-----------------------------
The existing server outcomes are shown in worker language. No coordinates
or debug values are ever shown.

- Within the area (location required, no exceptions): "Clocked in. Recorded
  at Chelth's server time. You're within the approved site area."
- Recorded with review: "Recorded. Your agency will review this
  attendance." (amber).
- Refusals keep the existing titles and the server message, plus a next
  step (guidance only; the rule stays the server's):
  - "Outside the site area" (OUTSIDE_GEOFENCE): move closer and try again,
    or contact your agency.
  - "Location not precise enough" (LOCATION_ACCURACY_TOO_LOW): wait for a
    stronger signal and try again.
  - "Location not available" / "Location needed for this site"
    (LOCATION_UNAVAILABLE / GEOFENCE_REQUIRED): allow location access for
    this site in the browser, then try again.
- Permission denied or unreadable: the existing notice ("Your location
  could not be read. Chelth will record the attempt without it."), now an
  amber note.
- No supervisor-override request was added: no such flow exists.


8. ACTIVE-SHIFT TREATMENT
-------------------------
After clock-in:
- the card chip becomes "Clocked in";
- the clock-in time shows in its tile;
- a green "Checked in" panel reads "Clocked in at <time> · shift <range>.
  Clock out when your shift is complete."
- Below it: "Clock out" (coral outline, exit glyph) and the existing "Start
  break".
- On a break: an "On a break" info panel ("You are on a break. End it
  before you clock out.") and "End break". Clock out is hidden, as before.

No live timer was added: none is supported.


9. CHECK OUT TREATMENT
----------------------
- The existing clock-out action. It is never blocked by location (verified
  in clock_out_assignment: geofence outcomes only open review exceptions).
- Success: "Clocked out." with "Recorded at Chelth's server time", or the
  amber "Recorded. Your agency will review this attendance." when
  exceptions were opened.
- The card shows both recorded times, "Worked <time> · breaks <time>",
  and, when exceptions exist, the amber "Review required" panel. That panel
  lists the existing exception labels (Early clock-out, Outside site area,
  etc.) and reads "Reviewed by your agency" once resolved.

No supervisor approval step was invented.


10. TIMESHEET HANDOFF
---------------------
After clock-out, a green "Attendance captured" panel reads: "Your worked
time goes to your weekly timesheet from this record — no need to type it
in."

When the worker's own timesheet week containing the shift's local date
exists (list_my_timesheets, which the worker's Timesheets page already
uses), the panel also shows:
- "Week <period>";
- the real timesheet status chip (Open / Submitted / …);
- "View timesheet", which opens the worker's own timesheet record ("My
  timesheet"). Submission stays there, with the existing flow.

No pay amounts are shown (worker pay visibility is deferred).

--------------
- There is no dedicated Shift Details route or separate Check-In screen.
  The reference's four screens are realised as the My Shifts card (with
  its in-card check-in states) plus the Shift Details sheet, because the
  existing product has one worker route.

  requirements are not in the worker read models, so they are omitted.

  truthfully: the server decides at the action. The outcome is shown
  after it.
- The worker Timesheets list and record pages and My Credentials inherit
  the restyled shell but kept their content styling (out of this phase's
  flow scope).
- BreakControl and the correction form keep their existing form styling
  inside the new cards.
