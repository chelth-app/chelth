1. EXISTING VOCABULARY AUDIT
----------------------------
"Regular" has two unrelated meanings in Chelth:

a) Shift classification, enum public.shift_classification
   ('regular', 'evening', 'night', 'weekend'):
   - stored on shifts.classification (default 'regular', fixed once the
     shift is open), rate_cards.classification and
     priced_timesheet_lines.classification;
   - used only to SELECT a rate (precedence tiers 1 and 3). It is never a
     money column.
   - The label "Regular" came from SHIFT_CLASSIFICATION_LABELS
     (src/lib/domain/pricing.ts). It is shown on:
     - Rates (card list, card summary, the rate form);
     - Pricing (queue issues, priced lines);
     - the shift record ("Regular shift");
     - the shift classification form.
   - It is not in any export (payroll, invoice and adjustment CSVs carry no
     classification).

b) "Regular time" in payroll / invoices = NON-OVERTIME minutes
   (pay_regular_minutes, total_regular_minutes, delta_regular_minutes).
   - This is overtime vocabulary, not a shift category, and is deliberately
     unchanged.

Not shift vocabulary at all: "400 Regular" (font weight) in the brand
guidelines.


2. REGULAR → DAY SHIFT DECISION
-------------------------------
'regular' is the standard daytime category and is used by rates, shifts,
priced lines, fixtures and tests. It is semantically safe, so the stored
value is KEPT and only the presentation changes:
- SHIFT_CLASSIFICATION_LABELS.regular = "Day Shift".
- Evening, Night and Weekend are unchanged and separate. Day Shift never
  covers them.
- New shiftClassificationPhrase() avoids "Day Shift shift" on the shift
  record ("Day Shift", "Night shift").
Pricing logic, rate precedence and every identifier are unchanged.


3. INTERNAL / DISPLAY MAPPING
-----------------------------
  stored 'regular' → "Day Shift"   'evening' → "Evening"
  stored 'night'   → "Night"       'weekend' → "Weekend"
All pickers, filters, rate cards, the pricing queue, priced lines and the
shift record read the single label map, so option text, visible text and
accessible names agree. Option values remain the enum keys.


4. EXISTING DURATION / RATE CALCULATION (documented, unchanged)
---------------------------------------------------------------
The only pricing engine is internal.price_timesheet_revision (P0-E7-S1,
calculation version 1, integers only). For each locked timesheet entry:
- worked minutes come from the immutable approval snapshot (breaks are
  already excluded; overnight shifts are a single entry of worked minutes);
- the rounding policy is chosen by work date. It is VERSIONED and
  effective-dated (rounding_policy_versions):
    mode 'none'    ⇒ priced minutes = worked minutes (EXACT MINUTES);
    mode 'nearest' ⇒ priced = round-half-up(worked / N) × N, with N in
                     {5, 6, 10, 15};
    no active policy ⇒ 'none';
- amount_minor = round-half-up(rate_minor × (regular × den + overtime × num)
  / (60 × den)), with round-half-up(a / b) = (2a + b) div 2b.
Payroll batches and invoice drafts read the priced lines. They never
recalculate. So pricing, payroll and invoices cannot diverge.

Current Chelth behaviour for an agency that never set a policy: exact
minutes. An agency with an active 'nearest' policy keeps it.


5. EXACT-MINUTE IMPLEMENTATION
------------------------------
No new setting and no engine change: "Exact minutes" IS the existing
rounding mode 'none'. Per §5 (extend, don't duplicate), it is now exposed
as a product setting:
- Settings → Rates & Billing → "Time Calculation Method":
  - In effect today (the latest active policy on or before today; none ⇒
    Exact minutes) and any scheduled change;
  - a worked example, for the method in effect: "Worked 7h 37m ·
    calculation basis 457 minutes · $30.00/hour = $228.50" (nearest 15:
    450 minutes · $225.00);
  - a form (rates.manage, AAL2; otherwise a step-up notice):
    - Exact minutes, or round to the nearest 5 / 6 / 10 / 15 minutes;
    - "Applies to work from" (default today);
    - it creates and activates a policy version through the existing RPCs.
      If activation is refused (for example an active policy already starts
      that day, CHM12), the draft is discarded and the error shown.
- Wording: describeRounding('none') is "Exact minutes" (was "No rounding")
  on Rates, Pricing and the rounding form.
- Explanation (§6): priced lines show Worked "7h 37m" with "457 min",
  Priced "<n> min · calculation basis", the rate, the amounts and the
  applied method. Pricing is pricing.view only. Workers never see
  pay / bill internals (unchanged).
Example check: 3000 × 457 / 60 = 22850 minor units = $228.50 exactly.


6. DECIMAL / MONEY SAFETY
-------------------------
- Stored money is computed only in Postgres, on integer minor units with
  numeric intermediates. No floating point.
- The Settings example uses amountForMinutes() with BigInt. It is display
  only and mirrors the engine formula (unit-tested to the same values).
- Rates are entered with the existing exact string → minor-unit parser.


7. FINANCIAL HISTORICAL BEHAVIOUR
---------------------------------
- Every priced line snapshots rounding_policy_version_id, rounding_mode,
  rounding_increment_minutes, raw_minutes and priced_minutes, plus the
  rate version. Priced timesheets are immutable; payroll batches and
  invoice drafts copy priced lines, then lock and finalise.
- A new method applies to work dated on or after its effective date, and
  only when a timesheet revision is (re)priced. Existing priced timesheets,
  locked or exported payroll and invoices, and adjustments are never
  recalculated.
- A corrected revision is priced as a new snapshot under the policy for its
  work dates. Corrections already flow through the existing adjustment
  workflow.
- Verified by the existing pgTAP 220 (P/Q: the old pricing is preserved
  after policy and rate changes; 453 → 450 under nearest-15).

10. LOCALE PRECEDENCE
---------------------
Workspace surfaces (any page under /app/organisations/<id>, including a
worker's own credential pages there):
  organisation locale → user locale → browser Accept-Language → en-US.
Personal surfaces (/app, Account, Security):
  user locale → browser → en-US.
- Only exact en-US / en-GB tags are recognised. en-AU, en-IE, fr-FR and
  others fall back to en-US. Nothing is guessed country by country, and
  physical location is never used.
- Locale and timezone are independent.
Storage (migration): organisations.locale and profiles.locale (NULL or
en-US / en-GB).
- Workspace: Settings → Organization → "Language & Spelling"
  (organisation.manage, AAL2, audited "organisation.locale_updated").
- Personal: Account → Language.
Existing workspaces have no locale, so they show the en-US fallback
("License"), or a member's own en-GB preference.


11. TIMEZONE ARCHITECTURE
-------------------------
Before: there was no user timezone. Operational times already used the
shift / facility timezone. Some non-operational timestamps (audit lists,
etc.) were formatted with no timezone, i.e. the server's zone (UTC).

Now, kept strictly separate:
- device / user (profiles.timezone_mode, profiles.timezone): personal
  DISPLAY preference only;
- organisation: Chelth has no organisation timezone (agency-wide dates are
  chosen explicitly, as before);
- facility / location: the authoritative operational calendar (unchanged);
- shift: follows its facility location (unchanged).
The personal zone is applied to the Settings → Security audit log (with the
zone name shown). Other non-operational timestamp lists still use the
server zone (section 23).


12. AUTOMATIC TIMEZONE BEHAVIOUR
--------------------------------
- Detection: Intl.DateTimeFormat().resolvedOptions().timeZone, validated
  (a real IANA zone; offsets and unknown names are rejected → null).
- DeviceTimezoneSync (in the /app layout): on load and whenever the app
  returns to the foreground, an AUTOMATIC profile with a changed, valid
  zone is saved through sync_my_device_timezone.
- The database updates only rows in automatic mode and validates against
  pg_timezone_names. A manual choice is never overwritten (enforced
  server-side, not only in the UI).
- Account → Timezone:
  - automatic: "Automatically detected · <zone>" [Change];
  - Change opens a searchable picker (a labelled text input with the full
    IANA list as suggestions, never a giant dropdown) → manual;
  - manual: "Timezone · <zone>" [Use device timezone] → back to automatic.
- No onboarding step asks for a timezone.


13. OPERATIONAL TIMEZONE PROTECTION
-----------------------------------
- shiftPeriod, the shift / attendance formatters and pricing work dates take
  the shift's own timezone and have no device or user timezone input. The
  display timezone cannot reach them.
- A 7:00 AM New York shift stays "7:00 AM" (New York time) on a phone in
  London. Unit-tested.
- No shift, attendance or pricing value is written from the device zone.


14. TODAY / UPCOMING / PAST VERIFICATION

16. EXACT FACILITY-PHOTO ROOT CAUSE
-----------------------------------
The uploader (E9-3D-S2) still had the same client-side traps that E9-3C
found and removed from the worker evidence uploader. Each one refuses a
real-device file BEFORE any network call, which matches 0 objects in
production:
a) A strict File.type equality to "image/jpeg" or "image/png". Android
   pickers commonly report "", "image/jpg" or application/octet-stream, or a
   name with no extension ⇒ refused.
b) A hard 2 MB client limit. Phone camera photos are typically 3–8 MB ⇒
   refused.
c) No selected state. A raw native file input sat beside an always
   identical outline "Upload photo" button. After a refused or no-op tap,
   nothing visibly changed beyond a small line of text, so the button read
   as inactive.
Permissions, Storage RLS, the bucket, the AAL2 gate and the server actions
were verified correct (the same flow succeeds locally, and production
configuration matches).
Not determinable without the device: which of (a), (b) or (c) the operator
hit. All three are removed, and real-device confirmation is listed in
section 23.


17. FACILITY-PHOTO FIX
----------------------
src/features/facilities/components/facility-image-uploader.tsx (rebuilt on
the E9-3C pattern):
- Empty: "Facility photo" / "Add a photo workers can use to recognise the
  facility." / "JPG or PNG · up to 2 MB (larger photos are resized)" /
  [Choose photo].
- Selected: file name, size and "Ready to upload"; primary [Upload photo];
  [Choose a different photo]. "resized for upload" is shown when that
  applied.
- Uploading: the button is disabled with aria-busy and a spinner. A ref
  guard stops duplicate submits.
- Success: the router refreshes, the page shows the preview, the control
  offers [Replace photo], and "Facility photo updated." is announced as a
  status.
- Errors (role="alert"):
  - wrong type, empty file, unreadable photo, too large even after resizing;
  - offline; network / Storage failure ("The photo was not uploaded. Check
    your connection and try again.");
  - server refusals (non-image bytes; permission or AAL2 from the ticket
    action).
  Nothing ever reports success early.
- checkFacilityImageFile() (unit-tested):
  - accepts image/jpg, image/pjpeg, x-png, an empty or octet-stream type
    with a .jpg / .png extension, and extension-less names;
  - refuses a known extension that contradicts the declared type.
- Photos over 2 MB are re-encoded in the browser (canvas → JPEG):
  - long edge ≤ 2000 / 1600 / 1200 px until ≤ 2 MB;
  - transparency is filled white;
  - camera metadata such as GPS is dropped.
- The native input stays in the DOM (sr-only, focusable, labelled) so Files,
  Photos and Camera remain available. The visible control is its label.
- Fallback (unchanged): no image ⇒ the approved Chelth facility artwork;
  workers never see a broken image.
