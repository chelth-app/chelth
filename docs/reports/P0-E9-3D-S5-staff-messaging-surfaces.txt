1. AGENCY SURFACE (S5.1)
------------------------
Routes, in the existing workspace shell (not redesigned):
- /app/organisations/<agency>/conversations (list);
- /app/organisations/<agency>/conversations/<thread>.

Navigation:
- "Messages" (new speech-bubble icon in the existing line family);
- listed only with message.view (capability-driven, the same predicate as
  the page gate);
- it sits after Timesheets in the Operations group.

Page gate: requireCapabilityOrNotFound(message.view) returns 404 without
it. The RPCs re-authorise every read and write.

Who holds message.view / send (S3):
- agency admin, operations manager, scheduler, recruiter, credentialing
  officer;
- NOT finance (tested: no nav item, 404).
Not every staff role gets every conversation automatically: access follows
the capability.

List:
- locked RecordPage, "All" / "Unread" tabs, a "Conversations" panel;
- each row shows:
  - the counterpart: the worker's name, or the facility for facility
    threads;
  - the context: "Worker conversation · <facility> · <date>" or "General";
  - the latest message;
  - the time (viewer's time zone);
  - an "<n> unread" chip.

Conversation:
- the counterpart as title, the context line linking to the agency's own
  shift record;
- messages with sender labels; the composer (reply) when the caller holds
  message.send, otherwise "You can read this conversation but not reply";
- marked read on open, refreshed while visible.


2. SHIFT / REQUEST CONTEXT (S5.2)
---------------------------------
Agency shift record:
- "Message <worker>" on each assigned worker opens the worker thread about
  that shift (requires message.send);
- "Message facility" opens the facility thread about that shift / request
  when the client facility is linked to a Chelth facility organisation and
  the shift is not a draft.

Agency worker record: "Message worker" (general thread).

Facility staffing request record: "Message agency" opens the facility
thread for that relationship and request.

The context link is preserved:
- the agency is taken to /shifts/<id>;
- the facility to its own /staffing-requests/<id>.
Truth is read from the shift, never copied.


3. FACILITY USERS (S5.3)
------------------------
- Facility admin and scheduler (message.view / send through the linked
  relationship) see ONLY facility threads of their relationships.
- They never see:
  - worker threads (tested: 404 on a worker thread URL; none listed);
  - other facilities or agencies;
  - agency notes;
  - staff personal names. The agency appears by organisation name.
- A facility supervisor without the capability gets no Messages item and a
  404 (tested).


4. INTERNAL NOTES SEPARATION (S5.4)
-----------------------------------
- Participant-visible messages are a separate model.
- Existing internal notes (shift_internal_notes, agency_worker_notes) are
  untouched, stay on their records, and never appear in threads; threads
  never appear in notes.
- No new internal-notes feature was invented.


5. HISTORY (S5.5)
-----------------
- Threads and messages are never deleted (S3 triggers).
- Tested: after the shift is marked completed, the conversation still shows
  the full history.
- Threads stay writable while the participants keep access. Access, not
  shift state, decides; a closed shift still often needs follow-up (for
  example timesheet questions).
- When a relationship ends, the facility side loses access automatically;
  the agency keeps the history.
