CHELTH — P0-E7-S2 PAYROLL PREPARATION & INVOICE DRAFTING
Stage report · 2026-10-01 · branch p0-e7-s2-payroll-invoices (from e5f4c0b, uncommitted)

======================================================================
1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
======================================================================
This stage adds the non-executing financial preparation layer on top of
the immutable pricing snapshots from P0-E7-S1.

  - Payroll periods: weekly or biweekly, calendar DATES with no timezone.
  - Payroll batches: draft → reviewed → approved → locked → exported, or
    cancelled before lock. There is never a "paid" state.
  - Invoice drafts: draft → reviewed → approved → locked → exported, or
    voided. There is never a sent, paid or overdue state.
  - Deterministic CSV exports for both, plus a DRAFT INVOICE PDF.
  - Export integrity and access: SHA-256 checksums, a private byte store,
    audited same-origin downloads.
  - Reconciliation states, and "Adjustment required" handling for revisions
    made after lock.

Lines are copied, never recomputed, from priced lines. A composite FK binds
every copied value to its immutable source. One-document-per-line is
enforced structurally by claim tables, and was proven under real concurrency.

Nothing pays, taxes, sends, collects, posts or integrates.

All validation passes. No HIGH or CRITICAL findings are open.

======================================================================
2. DOMAIN BOUNDARY
======================================================================
In scope: preparing payroll data, drafting internal invoices, generic exports.

Out of scope (not implemented, by design):
  - payroll execution;
  - ACH, direct deposit, bank transfers or bank files;
  - tax calculation, withholding or filing;
  - benefits, deductions, garnishments, payslips;
  - payment collection, card payments, Stripe, payment links;
  - QuickBooks, Xero or any accounting/GL integration;
  - remittance, claims, factoring, collections.

Legal boundaries are documented in PAYROLL_PREPARATION_MODEL §6:
  - preparation, not running payroll;
  - drafting, not collecting payment;
  - no tax;
  - no compliance guarantee;
  - generic exports are not certified integrations;
  - retention needs legal review.

======================================================================
3. PAYROLL PERIOD MODEL
======================================================================
agency_financial_settings:
  - payroll_period_type: weekly | biweekly;
  - payroll_week_starts_on: ISO weekday, checked against the anchor;
  - payroll_anchor_date: DATE;
  - reference prefixes.

Default when unset: weekly, starting on the agency's timesheet week start
(Monday unless configured).

Period of a facility-local work date d:
  start = d − (((d − anchor) mod L) + L) mod L, with L = 7 or 14.
This is pure date arithmetic; no timezone is involved.

payroll_periods rows are created on first use. An exclusion constraint
(btree_gist) forbids overlapping periods per agency, so a settings change
can never overlap used history (CHY05). A batch must start on a period
boundary (CHY05).

Settings are changed with set_agency_financial_settings, which requires
payroll.approve and invoice.approve (AAL2) and is audited.

======================================================================
4. PAYROLL BATCH SCHEMA
======================================================================
payroll_batches:
  - agency, period (composite FK to payroll_periods), currency;
  - reference PAY-YYYY-NNNNNN;
  - status;
  - line and worker counts, regular and overtime minutes, total_pay_minor
    (bigint);
  - created/reviewed/approved/locked/cancelled by (membership composite
    FKs) and at; exported_at; cancel_reason; status_changed_at.

Checks: status-dependent stamps, reference format.

A deferred constraint trigger proves at commit that the totals (count,
workers, minutes, pay) equal the sum of the lines.

Related tables: payroll_batch_lines (§5), payroll_line_claims (§16),
payroll_batch_history (append-only).

Worker totals are derived from the immutable lines
(list_payroll_batch_workers).

======================================================================
5. PAYROLL LINE SOURCE
======================================================================
Each payroll_batch_line stores:
  - source ids: priced line, priced timesheet, timesheet, revision, entry,
    worker, profile, facility, relationship;
  - discipline and work_date (facility-local);
  - regular and overtime minutes;
  - pay_rate_minor, pay_amount_minor, calculation_version;
  - display snapshots: worker reference and name, facility, discipline.

One composite FK covers all copied values and points to the new unique key
priced_timesheet_lines_payroll_source_key. A line therefore cannot hold a
value its source lacks, even when written by the owner (pgTAP J/L, 23503).

Eligibility:
  - the priced line is the current revision of a LOCKED timesheet;
  - it is unclaimed;
  - no other revision of that timesheet is claimed.

Lines are append-only. They can be inserted only while the batch is being
created (draft, same transaction).

======================================================================
6. PAYROLL LIFECYCLE
======================================================================
draft → reviewed → approved → locked → exported. Cancelled is reachable
from draft, reviewed or approved, and only before lock.

  prepare  create_payroll_batch(agency, period_start, currency)  payroll.prepare
  review   review_payroll_batch                                   payroll.prepare
  approve  approve_payroll_batch                                  payroll.approve (AAL2)
  lock     lock_payroll_batch                                     payroll.approve (AAL2)
  export   create_payroll_export                                  payroll.export (AAL2)
  cancel   cancel_payroll_batch(reason)                           payroll.prepare
                                                                  (payroll.approve once approved)

Cancelling releases the batch's lines; the cancelled batch keeps its lines
as history.

Every step:
  - appends to payroll_batch_history;
  - is audited.

A trigger enforces the transitions (CHY04).

======================================================================
7. PAYROLL APPROVAL & LOCK
======================================================================
Approve requires reviewed status; lock requires approved status. Both fail
closed (CHY02) while any line's source is superseded or its timesheet is
no longer locked.

After lock, protect_payroll_batch:
  - refuses any content change (CHY03);
  - allows only locked → exported;
  - writes each stamp once.

In addition:
  - lines refuse UPDATE, DELETE and TRUNCATE;
  - claims cannot be released (CHY03);
  - batches are never deleted.

Same-user prepare/approve is allowed (see §33).

======================================================================
8. PAYROLL EXPORT
======================================================================
The CSV is rendered in the database from the locked batch lines. Columns
are exactly as specified:

  batch_reference, worker_reference, worker_name, period_start,
  period_end, work_date, facility, discipline, regular_minutes,
  overtime_minutes, pay_rate_minor, pay_amount_minor, currency

Format:
  - RFC 4180, UTF-8 without BOM, CRLF;
  - text quoted; integers and dates bare;
  - ordered by line number.

Behaviour:
  - Only locked or exported batches can be exported.
  - The first export moves the batch to exported.
  - Re-exports are numbered and byte-identical.

The export snapshot (financial_exports) records:
  - format and export version (1);
  - row count, total, currency;
  - SHA-256 and byte size;
  - file_ref;
  - generated at/by;
  - source status at export.

The bytes are stored privately (§21).

======================================================================
9. EXPORT CHECKSUM
======================================================================
SHA-256 (pgcrypto digest) is computed over the exact stored bytes at
generation. It is then re-verified:

  - by trigger on file insert;
  - by download_financial_export before returning;
  - by the route (node:crypto, timing-safe) before streaming.

Any mismatch fails closed. The checksum is shown in the UI (short form plus
full value) and included in audit metadata.

Determinism is tested in pgTAP, integration (re-export gives an equal
checksum) and E2E (the downloaded file's SHA-256 equals the displayed
checksum).

======================================================================
10. INVOICE DRAFT SCHEMA
======================================================================
invoice_drafts:
  - agency, relationship and facility (composite FK to the relationship);
  - week (period_start/end, check +6), currency;
  - reference INV-DRAFT-YYYY-NNNNNN;
  - status;
  - line count, total priced minutes, total_bill_minor;
  - agency and facility name snapshots;
  - lifecycle stamps, void_reason.

A deferred totals check applies, as for batches.

Related tables: invoice_draft_lines, invoice_line_claims,
invoice_draft_history.

Numbering: an agency-configurable prefix plus a monotonic per-agency/year
sequence. This is an internal draft reference; legal invoice numbering is
deferred.

======================================================================
11. INVOICE LINE SOURCE
======================================================================
Each line copies only bill-side values: priced minutes, bill regular and
overtime minutes, bill_rate_minor, bill_amount_minor, revision and
calculation version.

Two FKs bind it:
  - a composite FK to priced_timesheet_lines_invoice_source_key;
  - (priced_timesheet_id, agency, period, currency) to priced_timesheets,
    which proves the line's week is the draft's week.

The invoice tables have NO pay or margin column (pgTAP K checks
information_schema). Pay data therefore cannot be stored, projected or
exported for a facility document.

Eligibility mirrors payroll; the adjustment hold is per (timesheet,
relationship).

======================================================================
12. INVOICE LIFECYCLE
======================================================================
draft → reviewed → approved → locked → exported. Voided is reachable from
any of these states.

  - Review: invoice.prepare.
  - Approve and lock: invoice.approve (AAL2).
  - Export (csv | pdf): invoice.export (AAL2).
  - Void: invoice.prepare before approval, invoice.approve after it. A
    reason is required.

Voiding never changes the lines or totals; it releases the claims.

There is no sent, paid or overdue state.

======================================================================
13. INVOICE APPROVAL & LOCK
======================================================================
This is identical to payroll:
  - approve and lock fail closed while a source is superseded (CHY02);
  - after lock, contents are immutable (CHY03);
  - only locked → exported and → voided are allowed (CHY04);
  - lines are append-only.

======================================================================
14. INVOICE EXPORT
======================================================================
CSV columns are exactly as specified:

  invoice_draft_reference, facility, relationship_reference,
  period_start, period_end, work_date, worker_reference, worker_name,
  discipline, priced_minutes, bill_rate_minor, bill_amount_minor, currency

relationship_reference is the relationship id.

PDF:
  - minimal PDF 1.4 with built-in Helvetica only;
  - titled "DRAFT INVOICE" on every page;
  - states "Not a tax invoice. Not a request for payment. No tax has been
    calculated.";
  - all text escaped;
  - no links, scripts, annotations, embedded files or remote resources
    (pgTAP).

Both files are named <reference>-DRAFT-INVOICE.(csv|pdf). Neither contains
pay values (pgTAP U, integration, E2E 4).

======================================================================
15. CURRENCY & MONEY
======================================================================
  - Integer minor units (bigint) throughout. There is no floating point in
    SQL, exports or tests.
  - A document has exactly one currency, enforced by composite FKs from
    lines to the document and to the priced source.
  - Totals are sums of copied line amounts; there is no re-rounding.

Money tests:
  - batch total = Σ priced pay totals;
  - draft total = Σ priced bill totals;
  - each line equals its source line (pgTAP, integration).

Worked case: 480 + 453 + 240 min at $42.50 / $58.00 gives
  - pay  $340.00 + $320.88 + $170.00 = $830.88;
  - bill $464.00 + $437.90 + $232.00 = $1,133.90.

======================================================================
16. DUPLICATE PREVENTION
======================================================================
Structural:
  - payroll_line_claims and invoice_line_claims each have the priced line
    id as PRIMARY KEY;
  - a claim is composite-FK-bound to its document line;
  - claims are insertable only during preparation;
  - they are deletable only when the document is cancelled or voided.

Preparation runs under a per-agency transaction advisory lock. A unique
violation maps to CHY10.

Tests:
  - pgTAP N/O: an owner-level duplicate claim gives 23505; re-preparing
    gives CHY01/CHY09.
  - Integration: two finance users run Promise.all; exactly one batch wins
    and the other gets CHY01/CHY10, with 3 claims all belonging to one
    batch. The same holds for invoice drafts.

======================================================================
17. REVISION / ADJUSTMENT HANDLING
======================================================================
Only the current revision of a locked timesheet is eligible; a superseded
priced revision is never "current" (pgTAP R).

When work already in a document gets a new revision:
  - Open document (draft, reviewed, approved): flagged SOURCE_SUPERSEDED.
    Approve and lock fail closed; the user cancels or voids and prepares
    again.
  - Locked or exported document: flagged "Adjustment required". The
    document is unchanged — lines, totals, history and export bytes alike
    (pgTAP S, integration, E2E 7). The newer revision is held back from
    ordinary preparation, so there is no double pay or bill. The queue
    references the earlier documents, e.g. "PAY-2026-000002 (exported)",
    and whether the new revision is priced.

Adjustment documents themselves are deferred (§34).

20. RLS ARCHITECTURE
======================================================================
Every new public table has RLS enabled, a SELECT grant only, and a policy
of authz.has_capability(agency, payroll.view | invoice.view). It is
deny-by-default and agency-only.

  - Workers, facilities and platform admins have no path (pgTAP C–F, X).
  - Writes happen only through SECURITY DEFINER RPCs with
    search_path = ''.
  - Projections re-check capability and return not-found (CHY06, CHY07,
    CHY08) to non-viewers, so there is no existence oracle.
  - internal.* helpers are executable by no API role.
  - The 000 baseline allow-list now includes the 30 new public RPCs.

21. FILE SECURITY
======================================================================
Bytes live in internal.financial_export_files:
  - private schema, RLS on;
  - no policies, no grants;
  - append-only;
  - checksum- and size-verified on insert.

There is no Supabase Storage object and no signed URL.

Downloads go through POST /app/exports/[exportId]/download:
  - same-origin only (403 otherwise);
  - signed-in only (401 otherwise).

The route calls download_financial_export, which:
  - checks *.export for the export's own agency at AAL2;
  - applies a rate limit;
  - re-verifies the checksum;
  - writes the audit row (payroll.export_downloaded /
    invoice.export_downloaded).

The route then re-verifies the SHA-256 and streams the file with:
  - an attachment disposition;
  - Cache-Control no-store;
  - nosniff;
  - an allow-listed content type and a validated file name.

Tested:
  - wrong tenant, worker, facility, scheduler: not found;
  - view-only member: CH403;
  - AAL1: CH402;
  - suspended member: refused;
  - signed out: 401;
  - cross-site: 403;
  - download audit row present.

"Expired URL" does not apply: no URL is minted, so every download is
re-authorised at request time. See FINANCIAL_EXPORT_SECURITY.md.

33. KNOWN LIMITATIONS
======================================================================
  - No adjustment or supplementary documents. A revision after lock is
    flagged and held; the difference is handled outside Chelth, using the
    shown references.
  - There is no segregation-of-duties enforcement between preparer and
    approver.
  - Denied download attempts are not audited.
  - PDF text supports Latin-1 only.
  - The invoice relationship_reference is the relationship UUID; there is
    no human relationship code.
  - A batch covers all eligible lines of a period and currency; there is no
    per-worker selection.
  - A settings change that would overlap used periods is refused rather
    than migrated.
  - Export bytes are held in Postgres. Fine for CSV/PDF at current scale;
    object storage may be needed for very large exports.
  - Retention of financial records and exports is indefinite pending legal
    review.
