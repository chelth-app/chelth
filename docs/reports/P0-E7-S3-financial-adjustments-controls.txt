CHELTH — P0-E7-S3 FINANCIAL ADJUSTMENTS & CONTROL HARDENING
Stage report · 2026-10-01 · branch p0-e7-s3-financial-adjustments (from main 8150e5b, uncommitted)

======================================================================
1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
======================================================================
Revisions made after lock are now resolved inside Chelth. When locked or
exported work is revised and priced again, finance prepares an adjustment:

  - a payroll adjustment (pay side), or
  - an invoice adjustment draft (bill side; an additional charge or a
    credit).

Both are delta-only and immutable. Each one:

  - compares the last financially accounted revision with the new priced
    revision;
  - matches lines by timesheet entry;
  - is chained revision by revision (1→2→3).

The originals never change: lines, totals, history, references, timestamps
and export bytes stay byte-identical, as tested.

Also delivered:

  - an optional maker/checker rule (preparer ≠ approver; default off);
  - audited, bounded refusals for approvals and downloads;
  - reconciliation with adjustment states;
  - deterministic, checksummed adjustment exports, including a DRAFT
    INVOICE ADJUSTMENT PDF;
  - operational queues on /payroll and /invoices.

Nothing pays, taxes, posts or issues. All validation passes, with zero
database-lint warnings and no unresolved HIGH or CRITICAL finding.

======================================================================
2. ADJUSTMENT DOMAIN
======================================================================
Explicit entities; normal documents were not reused:

  - payroll_adjustments, payroll_adjustment_lines, payroll_adjustment_claims,
    payroll_adjustment_history;
  - invoice_adjustments, invoice_adjustment_lines, invoice_adjustment_claims,
    invoice_adjustment_history.

Unit of adjustment:
  - payroll: one timesheet revision transition;
  - invoice: one (timesheet, facility relationship) revision transition.

Visibly distinct: references are PAY-ADJ-/INV-ADJ-, and pages are labelled
"Adjustment — not payment" and "Draft invoice adjustment — internal, not
sent".

======================================================================
3. REVISION LINEAGE
======================================================================
Each header stores:

  - timesheet (and relationship), from_revision → to_revision (to > from);
  - both priced timesheets, composite-FK-bound to (id, agency, timesheet,
    revision) and (id, currency);
  - the ROOT original document. Payroll: FK (id, agency, currency) to the
    batch. Invoice: FK (id, agency, relationship, facility, week, currency)
    to the draft;
  - previous_adjustment_id. Its FK proves previous.to_revision =
    from_revision, with the same timesheet (and relationship) and the same
    root.

The UI shows original document, "Follows" (previous adjustment), revision
change and source pricing links. Every link remains explainable months
later.

======================================================================
4. PAYROLL ADJUSTMENT SCHEMA
======================================================================
Header:
  - reference, status (payroll_batch_status), timesheet, worker/profile
    (FK to the timesheet);
  - period (FK to the to-priced timesheet);
  - revisions and both priced timesheets;
  - original batch, previous adjustment, currency;
  - line_count; signed regular/overtime minute deltas;
  - total_increase_minor ≥ 0, total_decrease_minor ≥ 0, net_delta_minor
    (CHECK: net = increase − decrease);
  - worker reference and name snapshots;
  - lifecycle stamps; cancel reason.

Lines (changed entries only):
  - entry, worker, facility, relationship, discipline, work date;
  - old_* and new_* pay sides: priced line id, priced timesheet, regular and
    overtime minutes, pay rate, pay amount, calculation version. Each side
    is composite-FK-bound to the priced line's pay-side source key, and is
    either wholly present or wholly NULL (num_nulls check);
  - signed deltas, CHECKed as new − old;
  - display snapshots.

There are no bill-side columns (pgTAP P).

======================================================================
5. INVOICE ADJUSTMENT SCHEMA
======================================================================
Header: as for payroll, plus:
  - relationship and facility (FK);
  - direction, a generated column (additional_charge / credit /
    no_net_change);
  - agency and facility name snapshots;
  - a void lifecycle.

Lines:
  - old and new bill sides: priced minutes, bill regular and overtime
    minutes, bill rate, bill amount, calculation version, each FK-bound to
    the priced line's bill-side key;
  - signed priced-minute and bill-amount deltas.

There are no pay or margin columns (pgTAP Q, integration).

======================================================================
6. DELTA CALCULATION
======================================================================
Sources:
  - Old side: the lines last financially accounted. These are the original
    document's claimed lines, or, after an adjustment, its whole
    to_revision.
  - New side: the current priced revision of the locked timesheet.

Matching: a FULL JOIN on timesheet entry (one per assignment, stable across
revisions); never names.

  - Added entry: old side NULL.
  - Removed entry: new side NULL, negative delta.
  - Changed: added, removed, or any of minutes / rate / amount differs. Only
    changed lines are stored.

Amounts are copied, never recomputed: there is no rate × minutes and no live
rates or attendance (pgTAP J; owner-level FK proof).

======================================================================
7. ADJUSTMENT CHAIN
======================================================================
The base is the latest non-cancelled/non-voided adjustment's to_revision,
otherwise the original's claimed revision. Each step compares against that
base and never back to revision 1.

Tested chain (pgTAP S, integration, E2E 4):

  revision 1 → original batch / draft;
  revision 2 → adjustment A (1→2, +$11.32 pay, +$15.10 bill);
  revision 3 → adjustment B (2→3, −$50.20 pay, −$61.75 bill), linked to A;
  revision 4 (financially identical) → resolved without a document.

Open adjustments:
  - one whose target is superseded blocks approve/lock (CHY02) until it is
    cancelled or voided;
  - a locked invoice adjustment cannot be voided, because it is a chain base.

======================================================================
8. POSITIVE / NEGATIVE HANDLING
======================================================================
  - Integer minor units throughout; signed bigint deltas.
  - Totals are increase and decrease magnitudes plus a signed net.
  - The UI shows "+$11.32 Increase", "−$50.20 Decrease", "+$15.10 Additional
    charge" and "−$61.75 Credit": a true minus sign plus a text label, never
    colour alone.
  - CSV numeric columns keep their sign.

Zero delta:
  - no material change → no document (CHY18), state no_change, resolved;
  - minutes or rate changed with a zero net → a document with net 0
    (direction no_net_change).

======================================================================
9. ORIGINAL DOCUMENT PRESERVATION
======================================================================
pgTAP H/I compare, before and after the adjustments:
  - the full batch row (to_jsonb);
  - every batch line;
  - the history count;
  - the original payroll CSV and draft-invoice PDF bytes (SHA-256 of the
    stored content);
  - the full draft row.

All are identical. The integration and E2E suites check the same.

Structural guards:
  - S2 immutability triggers;
  - adjustments only ever insert their own rows;
  - void_invoice_draft refuses an original that roots a chain (CHY21).

======================================================================
10. PAYROLL ADJUSTMENT LIFECYCLE
======================================================================
draft → reviewed → approved → locked → exported; cancelled before lock.

  prepare  create_payroll_adjustment(timesheet)    payroll.prepare
  review   review_payroll_adjustment               payroll.prepare
  approve  approve_payroll_adjustment              payroll.approve (AAL2) + maker/checker
  lock     lock_payroll_adjustment                 payroll.approve (AAL2)
  export   create_payroll_adjustment_export        payroll.export (AAL2)
  cancel   cancel_payroll_adjustment(reason)       payroll.prepare (payroll.approve once approved)

Each step appends to the history table and is audited. The trigger enforces
transitions and immutability after lock.

======================================================================
11. INVOICE ADJUSTMENT LIFECYCLE
======================================================================
draft → reviewed → approved → locked → exported; voided before lock.

  - invoice.prepare / invoice.approve (AAL2) / invoice.export (AAL2), as for
    payroll.
  - void_invoice_adjustment needs a reason and releases the transition
    claim.
  - There is no sent, paid, overdue or collected state.

Fixed during the stage:
  - The immutability trigger compared the generated `direction` column,
    which is NULL in NEW during BEFORE triggers. Every lifecycle update was
    refused. The column is now excluded from the comparison; it is derived
    from net_delta_minor, which is still compared.

15. MAKER / CHECKER POLICY
======================================================================
agency_financial_settings.financial_maker_checker_required, default false
(existing agencies are unaffected; pgTAP V).

  - Changed with set_financial_maker_checker, which requires

======================================================================
16. DENIED ACTION AUDITING
======================================================================
Audited refusals:
  - financial export download;
  - payroll batch approval and invoice draft approval;
  - payroll adjustment approval and invoice adjustment approval.

Each refusal records financial.action_denied:
  - metadata { attempted_action, reason_code } only;
  - reason codes NOT_FOUND, NOT_PERMITTED, MFA_REQUIRED, MAKER_CHECKER;
  - the organisation only when the actor is a member, so cross-tenant
    attempts never write into another agency's log;
  - no names, no free text, no content.

Refusals are RETURNED rather than raised, so the audit row commits:
  - approvals return (outcome, reason_code);
  - the download returns denied_reason.

Server actions fail closed unless outcome = 'approved'. State errors still
raise.

Bounds:
  - a per-actor cap of 60 per hour, checked FIRST;
  - then dedupe per (actor, action, target, reason) per 15 minutes.
Both audit rows and counters are bounded (see §29).

19. STRUCTURAL INTEGRITY
======================================================================
Composite FKs prove that:
  - the adjustment belongs to the agency;
  - the original document is the agency's and in the same currency (and,
    for invoices, the same relationship, facility and week);
  - both priced revisions belong to the same timesheet;
  - the previous adjustment ends at from_revision;
  - lines belong to the header (timesheet, revisions, currency);
  - each line side equals a pay-side (payroll) or bill-side (invoice)
    priced line of the right revision;
  - claims belong to the adjustment.

CHECKs:
  - signed deltas = new − old;
  - wholly present or wholly absent sides;
  - net = increase − decrease;
  - status stamps.

Deferred triggers: totals equal the lines.
Immutability: protect triggers; append-only lines and history; no TRUNCATE.

A currency mismatch is structurally impossible (pgTAP R, owner insert gives
23503).

29. SECURITY FINDINGS
======================================================================
No CRITICAL or HIGH findings remain.

Found and fixed during the stage:
  - [MEDIUM] Denial-audit counter growth. The dedupe bucket was consumed
    before the per-actor cap, so cycling random target ids could grow the
    rate-limit counter table without bound (audit rows were already
    capped). The cap is now checked first.
  - [MEDIUM] Invoice adjustment lifecycle refused all updates (generated
    column compared in a BEFORE trigger). This was fail-closed, not unsafe.
    Fixed.
  - [LOW] Mobile horizontal page overflow on financial pages (sr-only
    escapes); fixed for all financial tables. The same pattern exists on
    non-financial pages (attendance, timesheets, pricing, rates, shifts,
    workforce) and is deferred (§34).

Reviewed with no issue:
  - double counting (held states and claims);
  - chain errors (FK previous.to = from; base resolution);
  - original or export mutation (triggers; byte tests);
  - negative handling (CHECKs; signed only where intended);
  - cross-tenant IDOR (view checks; not-found);
  - pay leakage (no columns);
  - duplicate claims (PKs);
  - concurrency (lock plus PK);
  - maker/checker bypass (one membership per org; database-enforced);
  - denial metadata leakage (action and reason only);
  - direct writes (none);
  - service-role exposure (none; bundle check passes).

30. KNOWN LIMITATIONS
======================================================================
  - A transition whose accounted lines span several original documents
    (possible only with misaligned payroll periods) is refused ("split")
    rather than adjusted.
  - Invoice adjustments are internal drafts. Legal credit notes and
    invoice issuance remain outside Chelth.
  - A locked invoice adjustment cannot be voided; corrections continue the
    chain.
  - Maker/checker covers approval only (not lock or export), by design.
  - Refusals of lock, export creation and other non-approval actions still
    raise and are not audited (only approvals and downloads were in scope).
  - Adjustment exports are per adjustment; there is no combined multi-worker
    adjustment batch export.
  - The adjustment PDF supports Latin-1 text only (as in S2).
