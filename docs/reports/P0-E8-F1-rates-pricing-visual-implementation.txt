5. RATES — IMPLEMENTATION SUMMARY
---------------------------------
Header:
- "Rates" with the subtitle "Manage pay and bill rate structures used when
  pricing approved work."
- "+ Add Rate Card" (rates.manage only) reveals the existing create form
  (CSS :target, the same pattern as Facilities). The new card is saved as
  a draft.

KPI cards (counts over the loaded rate cards; each is a quick filter):
- Active Rate Cards: rate in force today; footer "N disciplines covered".
- Needs Attention: no rate in force today; footer "Blocks matching work".
  Per the engine, such a card blocks matching work with no fallback.
- Draft Versions: waiting to be activated.
- Upcoming Changes: a version starting later; footer "N ending within 30
  days", from the computed period end.

Filters:
- Facility scope: each facility, plus "All facilities (agency-wide)".
- Discipline.
- Shift type: each classification, plus "Any shift type".
- Status.
The filters narrow the loaded list; no new query. The earlier `?show=`
links still work.

Current Rate Cards table, one row per card:
- Facility, Discipline, Shift Type;
- Pay Rate and Bill Rate (in the version's own currency);
- Effective From and Effective To;
- Status: Active / Starts later / Draft / Not in force, with a
  "drafts · change upcoming" note;
- "⋯", which opens Rate Details.

Rate Details drawer:
- Overview tab:
  - Rate Details: pay, bill, currency, version, effective from / to, and
    the matching level (the engine tier, 1–4).
  - Pricing Rule note: "prices matching work", or "no rate in force today
    — no fallback".
- Versions tab:
  - the version table with phase chips;
  - the existing Activate / Discard forms on drafts;
  - the existing "New version" form (rates.manage).
- Footer: "View Pricing Queue" (pricing.view).

Rounding and Overtime: the same rules, policies and forms, now in a
RefPanel with a locked table and empty state.

Pagination is unchanged (first 50 cards; "Next rate cards").

Reference elements deliberately not built, because they are not supported
by Chelth:
- Missing Rates as role × facility combinations;
- Expiring Soon as a separate status;
- Rate Exceptions;
- weekend and night differentials;
- Export;
- Duplicate Rate;
- Deactivate (versions are superseded, never deactivated);
- View Related Shifts (no rate-to-shift relation);
- Usage & Related Data metrics;
- per-rate audit history;
- Recent Pricing Activity (no rate activity feed).
The reference's "Edit Rate" is the existing new-version flow, so history
is never rewritten.


6. PRICING — IMPLEMENTATION SUMMARY
-----------------------------------
The Pricing route is the real pricing queue: locked timesheets that are
blocked, ready or priced. Rate configuration lives on Rates. The page
therefore explains how rates are matched and keeps the queue.

Subtitle: "How Chelth determines the pay and bill rates for approved work,
and the timesheets waiting to be priced." The brief's "Configure how…"
wording was not used, because nothing is configured on this page.

KPI cards (each is a quick filter to its queue):
- Needs Attention: footer "N missing rates" (RATE_NOT_CONFIGURED issues).
- Ready for Pricing.
- Priced: footer "N superseded by a revision".
- Rate Cards in Force: shown only with rates.view granted; links to Rates.
Queue counts show "50+" once a queue fills its first page (50).

How Rates Are Matched (the Settings explanatory pattern):
- The four engine tiers in order:
  1. facility + discipline + shift type;
  2. facility + discipline, any shift type;
  3. all facilities + discipline + shift type;
  4. all facilities + discipline, any shift type.
- With rates.view, each tier shows the count of rate cards at that level.
- An amber "No fallback to a broader rate" note: if the first matching card
  has no version in force on the work date, the timesheet waits in Needs
  attention; priced timesheets keep their rates.
- "Manage rate cards" in the header.
This text describes internal.resolve_rate; the logic is unchanged.

Queue:
- SectionTabs (Needs attention / Ready for pricing / Priced).
- A RefPanel with a REF_TABLE: worker avatar and name (a link for priced
  rows), week, facilities, worked time, pay / bill or status.
- Issue chips with date and shift type.
- The existing Price / Price again forms and the "Add or activate a rate"
  link, which now opens Rates filtered to Needs attention.
- Pagination unchanged.

Priced timesheet record (canonical record arrangement):
- Back link "Pricing".
- Title: worker name. Subtitle: week · timesheet revision.
- Header meta: a "Current snapshot" or "Superseded by revision N" chip,
  then "Priced <when> by <name> · Calculation version N".
- Section tabs: Summary and Priced Shifts.
- Summary card: worked, priced time, pay, bill, derived margin, currency
  and lines.
- Priced Shifts card: the per-line table, unchanged, plus the amounts
  note.

  of each queue, labelled as such ("first 50", "50+"). There are no
  aggregate count RPCs, and none were added.

  relationship, not the agency facility id.

  review.

  behaviour as Timesheets.

  were not imitated.
