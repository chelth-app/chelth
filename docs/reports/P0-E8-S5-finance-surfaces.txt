======================================================================
1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
======================================================================
Every agency finance surface now uses the shared Chelth P3-F / P4 language:
  - Rates;
  - Pricing;
  - Priced timesheet;
  - Payroll, Payroll batch, Payroll adjustment;
  - Invoices, Invoice draft, Invoice adjustment.

Changes:
  - PageHeader on every page.
  - A "Payroll | Invoices" and "Rates | Pricing" mode switch (route links
    between existing pages; shown only when the caller can open both).
  - Quick-filter KPI cards from counts the pages already load.
  - URL-backed status filters.
  - DataTable primitives on every finance table.
  - StatusChip for lifecycle, attention, reconciliation and rate-version
    states.
  - KeyValueList for totals, lineage and net change.
  - ActivityTimeline for immutable history.
  - Read-only preview drawers on the batch and draft lists.

Finance stays preparation, approval and export only:
  - no payment, sending, collection or posting vocabulary or controls;
  - the existing disclaimers ("Adjustment — not payment", "Draft invoice —
    internal, not sent", "Not a request for payment", "No tax calculated")
    are kept, and now also appear as header metadata.

No database, migration, RLS, capability, Server Action, query, RPC,
calculation, precedence, maker/checker, export or reconciliation logic
changed.

21. KNOWN VISUAL DIFFERENCES
======================================================================
1. No Sent / Paid / Overdue / ACH / Outstanding states, "Payment received"
   rows or "Process Payroll"; the primary action is "Prepare a batch" /
   "Create a draft".
2. No date-range control in the header. Payroll periods come from settings;
   the batch list filters by status.
3. No KPI deltas ("↑ 12 from last period"), worker avatars, row checkboxes,
   bulk actions or kebab menus.
4. No docked right-hand drawer. Previews are modal (locked) and read-only;
   lifecycle actions live on the record page.
5. No "Rate Information" panel in the payroll drawer: rates are per line on
   the batch page.
6. No Pricing KPI cards (the queue query is capped at 50).
7. The invoice-adjustment lineage and net lists keep their own list markup
   (restyled), not KeyValueList.
8. Long metadata labels (e.g. "Draft invoice — internal, not sent") wrap on
   phones.
