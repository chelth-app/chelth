2. CREDENTIALS & COMPLIANCE CHANGES
-----------------------------------
Card 1 — Credential Requirement Rules (kept; stronger hierarchy):
- A shorter baseline explanation, with an "n active" chip in the header
  (as Team & Permissions).
- Requirement rows on the canonical record list: icon tile, requirement
  name (15 / 600), and scope meta (discipline or "All workers",
  jurisdiction, effective-from date, minimum validity).
- Chips: "Must be verified" / "Verification not required" and "Warn N days
  before expiry".
- An "n inactive kept for history" note when there are inactive
  requirements.
- Management links as canonical outlined actions (44 px): "Manage baseline
  requirements" (Compliance) and "Manage facility requirements"
  (Facilities).

Card 2 — How Readiness Is Calculated (new; informational, no controls).
The engine's real inputs, each a record row with an icon tile:
- agency baseline requirements (every worker or one discipline, from the
  effective date);
- facility-specific requirements (on the facility record, only for work
  there);
- worker credential evidence (submitted and shared; documents count only
  once the security scan clears them);
- verification status (your agency's decision where the requirement must
  be verified);
- expiration and effective dates (valid on the shift's local date, minimum
  validity, warning window).
It also shows the three real results (Ready / Action required / Not
eligible) and the links "View Compliance" and "Manage Facility
Requirements".

Card 3 — Related Compliance Areas (new; navigation only):
- Compliance, Facilities (facility.view) and Workforce (worker.view): an
  icon tile, title and one-line purpose each.
- No metrics, charts or decorative filler.

Accessibility fix: the old inline text links were 21 px tall. With the
account menu open at 768 px, axe flagged target size against the menu's
"Sign out" row. The links are now 44 px actions, and axe is clean with the
menu open at every width.


3. INFORMATION USED
-------------------
- listRequirements (active / inactive, credential type, discipline,
  jurisdiction, must-be-verified, minimum validity, warning window,
  effective-from);
- listCredentialTypes and listDisciplines (names);
- READINESS_LABELS.
- The readiness explanation restates existing engine behaviour,
  corresponding to the engine reasons NOT_SUBMITTED, CREDENTIAL_NOT_SHARED,
  DOCUMENT_NOT_CLEARED, UNVERIFIED_CREDENTIAL, EXPIRED_CREDENTIAL and
  INSUFFICIENT_VALIDITY, and the per-shift local-date evaluation.
- No new data or configuration.


4. ROUTES REUSED
----------------
/app/organisations/[id]/compliance (and #baseline-heading), /facilities and
/workforce. All exist, and each is shown only where the caller holds the
capability.


5. SETTINGS ICON NORMALIZATION
------------------------------
Sub-navigation icons, which read thinner and lighter than the sidebar's:
- size: 20 → 21 px (the sidebar uses 22 px, so the subnav stays
  subordinate);
- stroke: 2 → 1.9 on the 24 px grid;
- inactive colour: slate-500 → navy at 80%; active: teal-dark;
- alignment: a fixed 22 px icon column; label gap 12 → 14 px.
Icon meanings are unchanged.


6. ACCOUNT DROPDOWN REFINEMENT
------------------------------
Behaviour, ids, labels, links, forms and order of actions are unchanged.
"All workspaces" is shown once, in the navigation group.

Surface:
- white; 12 px radius (the card radius family);
- a teal 14% hairline;
- canonical drawer elevation (two-layer navy shadow);
- 336 px wide (max: viewport − 2 rem); no translucency.

Groups, separated by teal 10% hairlines:
- A. Identity: 40 px initials avatar, name (15 / 600 ink), email
  (12.5 slate), on a soft mint-white band.
- B. Current workspace: an uppercase section label, then a descriptive
  (non-interactive) tile with a teal check circle, the workspace name
  (14 / 600) and type · role (· Suspended). It is not styled as a choice.
- Switch workspace (when other memberships exist): rows unchanged in
  content.
- C. Navigation: All workspaces, Account and Security, each with a
  teal-dark icon.
- D. Sign out: separated and restrained (slate text that darkens on hover);
  not red.

Rows: 44 px, 8 px radius, a subtle teal-tinted hover and focus fill
(mint-mist 70%). The global :focus-visible ring is kept. No blue
highlight and no glow.


7. ACCESSIBILITY
----------------
- Disclosure semantics are unchanged: a button with aria-expanded and
  aria-controls; links and buttons in tab order; the "Current workspace"
  group label; the "Switch workspace" list label.
- Escape closes the menu and returns focus to the trigger (asserted at
  four widths). An outside click or focus leaving the menu closes it.
- The Settings nav keeps aria-current="page".
- axe (wcag2a / 2aa / 22aa) is clean on Credentials & Compliance and with
  the menu open, at 1440 / 1920 / 768 / 412.


8. RESPONSIVE RESULTS
---------------------

  chips under the name on narrow widths.
- Account menu at 1440 / 1920 / 768 / 412: the same anchored panel the
  shell already uses, clamped to the viewport on phones. No new mobile
  pattern.
