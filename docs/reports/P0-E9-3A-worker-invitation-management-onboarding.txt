1. EXACT ROOT CAUSE OF THE DUPLICATE-INVITE CONFLICT
---------------------------------------------------
public.create_organisation_invite always INSERTed a new row.
- The partial unique index organisation_invites_one_pending
  (organisation_id, email) WHERE status = 'pending' rejected a second
  pending invitation with SQLSTATE 23505.
- The app mapped 23505 to the generic CONFLICT ("This change conflicts
  with the current state. Please refresh and try again.").
- Expiry is computed (status stays 'pending' after expires_at), so an
  expired, never-accepted invitation blocked re-inviting in the same way.
- The "already a member" case was also generic (CH409 → "not allowed in
  the item's current state").

Classification:
- RPC logic: primary.
- Constraint behaviour: the trigger.
- Error mapping: hid the specific state.
- UI logic: not the cause.

Fix: migration 20261009110000_invite_reissue_pending (section 12):
- Same email + same role while pending: the existing invitation is
  re-issued through the rotation semantics (token rotated, old hash
  overwritten, fresh 7-day expiry, 5-send cap, `invite.resent` audit). No
  second row.
- Different role pending: CHI09 → "This email already has a pending
  invitation with a different role. Revoke that invitation first, then
  invite again."
- Already a member: CHI10 → "This person is already a member of this
  organisation." In Workforce: "This person is already part of your
  workforce."
- Concurrent issues for the same person are serialised with a
  transaction-scoped advisory lock.
- Truly unexpected 23505 / CH409 keep the generic fallback.


2. ADMIN PENDING INVITATIONS IMPLEMENTATION
-------------------------------------------
- Workforce gains a distinct "Pending Invitations" RefPanel
  (workforce/_components/pending-invitations.tsx) after the worker list.
- Pending invitations are never rendered as worker rows.
- Data: the existing list_organisation_invites RPC (membership.invite at
  AAL2; tenant-scoped; returns no token or hash), filtered to
  agency.healthcare_worker invitations with status `pending`.
- Row:
  - email (wraps);
  - "Healthcare Worker · Invited <date> · Expires/Expired <date>";
  - chips: Pending (info) or Expired (warning, computed from the
    invitation's own expires_at);
  - the real delivery status: Emailed / Not emailed / Email failed / Not
    sent yet;
  - actions: Resend and Cancel invite.
- Count: "N pending invitations" in the panel header.
- Empty: LockedEmpty "No pending invitations."
- Shown only when the caller holds membership.invite at AAL2 (the invite
  form's own gate). Otherwise the existing step-up notice applies.
- Locked visual family reused: RefPanel, RecordList / RECORD_ROW, RefChip,
  LockedEmpty, outlined secondary actions (44 px on phones).
- No Workforce redesign.


3. RESEND BEHAVIOUR
-------------------
Uses the existing resendInviteAction → resend_organisation_invite:
- token rotated; old hash overwritten (old link dead);
- expiry reset to 7 days (also renews an expired pending invite);
- send_count + 1 (cap 5 → CH429);
- `invite.resent` audited; delivery state reset by the existing trigger;
- the email is sent post-commit through the configured provider;
- the outcome is recorded.

Success copy:
- "Invitation resent to <email>. The previous invitation link no longer
  works."
- If not emailed: "Invitation re-issued for …" plus the one-time link.

Re-submitting "Invite worker" for a pending email takes the same path
(section 1).


4. CANCEL BEHAVIOUR
-------------------
"Cancel invite" uses the existing revokeInviteAction →
revoke_organisation_invite:
- the token is unusable immediately;
- the row is kept (status revoked; never deleted);
- `invite.revoked` is audited;
- the invitation leaves the Pending list.

The confirmation "Invitation cancelled. <email> can no longer use the
invitation link." is shown by a panel-level notice (InviteNoticeProvider).
The cancelled row disappears on re-render, so a per-row message would never
be seen; E2E caught this. Settings → Team keeps its existing "Revoke"
control. No invitation history product was built.


5. ACCEPTED TRANSITION
----------------------
- The accepted invitation leaves Pending (status accepted).
- The existing acceptance creates the membership with the fixed role, and
  the existing trigger creates exactly one agency_workers record (pgTAP
  plus integration).
- The worker appears in the Workforce list in the existing onboarding
  status. Nothing is auto-activated.


6. WORKER INVITE LANDING (signed out; oracle-free)
--------------------------------------------------
/invite is identical for valid, invalid and missing tokens:
- "You've been invited to join Chelth";
- "Create or sign in to your account to review and accept your workforce
  invitation.";
- three steps;
- primary "Create account";
- "Already have a Chelth account? Sign in" (→ /sign-in?next=/invite).
No agency, email, role or validity is shown (asserted in E2E). AuthCard /
AuthPanel locked family.


7. NEW-ACCOUNT FLOW
-------------------
invite link (token → httpOnly cookie, 303 to /invite) → Create account →
sign up → confirmation email → /auth/confirm verifies.

New: if an invite cookie is present and no explicit destination was given,
/auth/confirm redirects to /invite instead of the /app gateway. Password
recovery is never diverted, and a failed verification never resumes.

The authenticated review follows:
- "Join as a healthcare worker";
- "<Agency> has invited you to join their workforce.";
- Agency, Role, Invited email, Expires;
- Accept invitation (primary, full width) and "Not now".

Accept → the existing accept_organisation_invite (token from the cookie
only; role and organisation from the row; verified email must match).

New post-accept destination: a self-service-only member with a worker
record (the worker frame's own rule) lands on
/app/organisations/<agency>/my-shifts in the WorkerShell. Staff land on the
organisation as before.


8. EXISTING-ACCOUNT FLOW
------------------------
invite → "Sign in" → /sign-in?next=/invite → review → accept → My Shifts
(E2E).

Wrong account (signed in as B, invite for A): the database resolves no
invitation for B. B sees the uniform "Invitation unavailable" message
(which includes "…or was sent to a different email address"), with no
agency name and no Accept button (E2E). The message stays uniform,
following the threat model's uniform-response rule.


9. INVITE-CONTEXT PERSISTENCE
-----------------------------
- The existing httpOnly, SameSite=Lax invite cookie is reused. Its
  lifetime was raised from 1 h to 24 h so it survives sign-up and email
  confirmation.
- The token itself stays single-use, email-bound, and expires in 7 days.
- The token is never in localStorage, sessionStorage, query strings or
  client state.
- /auth/confirm reads the cookie from the request (pendingInviteTokenFrom),
  not from client input.
- Cross-device (confirming on another device or browser) cannot resume
  automatically without an oracle. The /app pending banner and re-opening
  the emailed link still work (known gap).


10. MULTI-AGENCY HANDLING
-------------------------
- A worker of Agency A accepts Agency B's invitation: one profile, two
  memberships, two agency_workers rows (integration).
- Being a worker elsewhere is not "already part of your workforce": the
  member check is per organisation.
- Post-accept routing targets the accepting agency only; other
  memberships are untouched.


11. EMAIL BEHAVIOUR
-------------------
The existing transactional invite email is unchanged. It already reads
"You have been invited to join <Agency> on CHELTH as <Role>" with "Accept
invitation" → <origin>/invite/<token>.

The direct post-commit delivery path (not the notification outbox) is
preserved, along with:
- the recorded delivery_status;
- the one-time fallback link when delivery is skipped or failed (never
  shown after a successful send; unit-tested);
- no logging of the recipient, token or URL.

19. KNOWN GAPS
--------------
- Cross-device confirmation cannot auto-resume (no oracle by decision).
  Re-opening the email link or the /app banner recovers.
- Sign-up email is not prefilled (by decision). The server-side email
  binding still prevents a mismatched acceptance.
- "Not now" only clears the local invite cookie. There is no worker-side
  decline RPC, and the invitation stays pending until it expires or the
  agency cancels it.
- Wrong-account and expired messages remain the uniform "invitation
  unavailable" text (threat-model uniform responses). Expiry is visible
  to admins in Pending.
- Revoked or accepted invitations are not shown in Workforce. Settings →
  Team still lists recent ones (no new history product).
- The invitation email copy is unchanged (already adequate).
