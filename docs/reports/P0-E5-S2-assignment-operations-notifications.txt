CHELTH — P0-E5-S2 ASSIGNMENT OPERATIONS & NOTIFICATIONS
Stage report · 2026-09-30 · branch p0-e5-s2-assignment-operations (uncommitted)

======================================================================
1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
======================================================================
Chelth now works when nobody is watching the dashboard.

  - Notification delivery. Domain events are queued per recipient in the
    same transaction as the change that caused them. A dispatcher route
    delivers them through the provider-neutral email layer (Resend in
    production). Delivery properties:
      * two dispatchers can never send the same row (claims use
        FOR UPDATE SKIP LOCKED plus a lease and a claim token);
      * retries are bounded (1 min, 5 min, 30 min, 2 h, then failed);
      * every row has a stable provider idempotency key;
      * nothing sensitive is stored or audited.
    The dispatcher uses a database role that can execute exactly two
    functions. No service-role key is involved.
  - Readiness monitoring. Upcoming assignments are re-evaluated hourly, and
    on demand, by the canonical eligibility function. Assignments that
    become ineligible open auditable "issues" and notify agency operations
    once. Workers cannot accept an ineligible assignment. Nothing is
    silently cancelled.
  - Relationship changes. Suspending or ending a relationship applies its
    operational effects in one transaction:
      * new work is blocked immediately;
      * upcoming work is flagged, and live offers are cancelled;
      * ending also cancels not-yet-started shifts.
    History is kept, and the audit records counts only.
  - Shift offers. Agencies can broadcast a shift to chosen eligible
    workers. Acceptance re-runs the full live gate (compliance, schedule,
    capacity). Offers never overfill a shift: once headcount fills, the
    remaining offers close. Facilities see no offer activity.
  - Scale. Shift lists use keyset pagination. Candidate evaluation
    pre-filters structurally impossible workers with set-based SQL and runs
    the compliance engine only for the rest.
  - UI. Offers on the shift page and on /my-shifts; a "Needs attention"
    badge and a "Re-check readiness" button; a new /operations page.

Validation:
  - pgTAP: 756 assertions (138 new).
  - Integration: 68 tests (11 new).
  - E2E: 66 runs across desktop and mobile (10 new).
  - Unit: 166 tests (14 new).

There are no unresolved HIGH or CRITICAL findings.

======================================================================
2. NOTIFICATION ARCHITECTURE
======================================================================
End-to-end flow:

  domain RPC
    → internal.enqueue_notification (same transaction)
    → per-recipient outbox row (ids and codes only)
    → pg_cron kick every minute
    → pg_net POST /api/internal/notifications/dispatch (Bearer secret)
    → claim (the address and template data are resolved at this point)
    → renderNotification
    → EmailSender (Resend)
    → complete.

Separation of concerns:
  - Domain code depends only on the event, the recipient identity and the
    template data.
  - Only getEmailSender() knows about Resend.
  - SMS, push or in-app delivery would be new senders; they are not built.

Recipient policy (the narrowest safe default):

  Recipient            Who exactly
  -------------------  ---------------------------------------------------
  worker               the one worker concerned
  agency operations    active members holding assignment.manage (at most
                       25), excluding the actor
  facility             active members of the LINKED facility holding
                       shift.request

All current events are required operational notifications.
Document: docs/architecture/NOTIFICATION_DELIVERY.md.

======================================================================
3. OUTBOX CONSUMER
======================================================================
Route: POST /api/internal/notifications/dispatch (Node runtime, no-store).

Guards:
  - It requires a bearer secret, compared in constant time over SHA-256
    digests.
  - With configuration missing it returns 503 not_configured.
  - With the email provider disabled it returns email_disabled and claims
    nothing, so rows wait until a provider is configured.

Database access: NOTIFICATION_WORKER_DATABASE_URL, a login role inside
chelth_notification_worker. That role may EXECUTE only
internal.claim_notifications and internal.complete_notification. It has no
table privileges and cannot bypass RLS. pgTAP pins this.

Batching: batches are bounded (default 25, maximum 100), with a 120 s lease.

Dispatcher code: src/lib/notifications/dispatcher.ts. It has pure
orchestration with an injected store and sender, and logs ids and codes
only.

Triggering: migration-scheduled pg_cron job chelth-notification-kick. It
calls pg_net only when rows are due, and only when the Vault URL and secret
exist; otherwise it is a no-op. The documented alternative is Vercel Cron
(Pro).

======================================================================
4. DELIVERY STATE MODEL
======================================================================
internal.notification_state has five values:

  State        Meaning
  -----------  ----------------------------------------------------------
  pending      queued
  processing   claimed, with a claim_token and a claimed_until lease
  sent         delivered; records sent_at and provider_message_id
  retry        due again at next_attempt_at
  failed       final, with a safe last_error_code

Constraints: sent ⇔ sent_at, and processing requires a token and a lease.

Claiming:
  - It uses SELECT … FOR UPDATE SKIP LOCKED inside the claim function.
  - A second claim in flight gets nothing.
  - An expired lease makes the row reclaimable and counts as an attempt.
  - A completion carrying a stale token is ignored (stale_claim).

At claim time, a row is failed with a code instead of being sent when:
  - the recipient is no longer an active, confirmed member of the audience
    organisation (recipient_unavailable);
  - the subject is gone or belongs to the wrong tenant (subject_unavailable);
  - it is past deliver_until (expired_before_delivery).

Rows left over from P0-E5-S1 were organisation-level and had no recipient.
They were closed as legacy_undelivered, not sent late.

======================================================================
5. RETRY / IDEMPOTENCY MODEL
======================================================================
Retries:
  - Backoff: 1 min, 5 min, 30 min, 2 h. The 5th failed attempt is final.
    These values are configurable in internal.operations_settings.
  - Permanent failures: provider_rejected (for example an invalid
    recipient) and template_invalid.
  - Temporary failures: unavailable, unreachable, rate-limited and auth.
    Auth is treated as temporary because it is fixed by correcting
    configuration.

Idempotency:
  - Key: chelth-notification-<outbox id>, stable across retries.
  - A send that succeeded but wasn't recorded is de-duplicated by Resend
    within its 24 h window. The whole retry schedule (under 3 h) fits
    inside it.
  - A unique index allows one open row per (event, subject, recipient).

Guarantee: at least one delivery attempt per row, de-duplicated by the
provider key. Limits (documented): a provider that ignores keys, or a
resend after 24 h, could duplicate.

======================================================================
7. ASSIGNMENT NOTIFICATION FLOW
======================================================================
Worker-facing:
  - Direct assignment → worker_assigned to the worker (deliver until the
    shift ends).
  - The agency cancels an assignment → assignment_cancelled.
  - The agency cancels a shift → shift_cancelled to each active worker.
  - Ending a relationship cancels not-yet-started shifts →
    shift_cancelled.

Agency-facing:
  - A worker declines → assignment_declined to agency operations.
  - An offer is made → shift_offered to that worker (deliver until the
    offer expires).

Not emailed (visible in the UI instead): offer declines, offer
acceptances, and a worker accepting an assignment.

======================================================================
8. FACILITY REQUEST NOTIFICATION FLOW
======================================================================
  - A facility submits a request → facility_request_submitted to the
    agency's assignment.manage users.
  - The agency opens it → facility_request_opened to the linked facility's
    shift.request users. The link points to the facility's own route.
  - A facility request is cancelled by the agency → shift_cancelled to the
    facility.

Tenant isolation: delivery rows belong to the recipient's organisation.
The template refuses a facility audience that isn't the shift's linked
facility.

======================================================================
9. ASSIGNMENT READINESS MONITORING
======================================================================
internal.run_assignment_readiness_scan:
  - Runs hourly through pg_cron (chelth-readiness-scan).
  - Horizon: from settings (default 14 days, configurable from 1 h to 60
    days).
  - Covers active assignments on open shifts that haven't ended and start
    within the horizon, plus any assignment with an open issue.
  - Bounded, idempotent, and recorded in internal.scheduled_job_runs.

Manual re-check: public.recheck_shift_readiness (audited).

Policy:
  - READY → no action.
  - Not ready and not yet accepted → an issue, a notification to agency
    operations, and the worker cannot accept.
  - Not ready and already accepted → the assignment is kept and flagged
    URGENT.
  - Assignments are never auto-cancelled.

Document: docs/architecture/ASSIGNMENT_READINESS_MONITORING.md.

======================================================================
10. ASSIGNMENT ISSUE MODEL
======================================================================
public.assignment_issues holds:
  - issue_type: not_eligible or relationship_not_active;
  - severity: attention, or urgent when the assignment is accepted or
    starts within 48 h;
  - block codes and compliance codes (reason codes only);
  - detected_by, opened_at, last_evaluated_at, resolved_at and resolution.

Rules:
  - At most one open issue per assignment and type.
  - Resolved issues are immutable, and nothing is deleted.
  - Composite foreign keys tie the issue to its tenant, and a trigger
    checks that the assignment belongs to the shift.

Audit: assignment.issue_opened and assignment.issue_resolved.

======================================================================
11. RELATIONSHIP SUSPENSION WORKFLOW
======================================================================
set_facility_relationship_status is redefined to run as ONE transaction.

On suspension:
  - New work is blocked (CHS10).
  - Live offers are cancelled.
  - Upcoming active assignments are flagged.
  - Affected shifts are listed on /operations.
  - Agency operations are notified.

On ending: the same, plus not-yet-started shifts are cancelled (reason
relationship_ended). Their assignments are cancelled and the workers
notified. Shifts already in progress are kept and flagged.

On reactivation: relationship issues are resolved.

Audit: relationship.status_changed (both organisations) and
relationship.operations_applied (counts only).

Concurrency: the status change locks the relationship FOR UPDATE; every
work-creating path takes a FOR SHARE lock first. So new work either commits
before the suspension (and is flagged by it) or is refused.

Document: docs/architecture/RELATIONSHIP_SUSPENSION_OPERATIONS.md.

======================================================================
12. SHIFT OFFER MODEL
======================================================================
public.shift_offers moves from offered to one of: accepted, declined,
expired, or cancelled (reason shift_filled, shift_cancelled, shift_closed,
withdrawn, relationship_not_active or assigned_directly).

Creating offers: offer_shift_to_workers.
  - Offers go to 1–50 workers.
  - Expiry: 15 minutes to 7 days, capped at the shift start.
  - Allowed only for an open shift that hasn't started and isn't full,
    under an active relationship.
  - A foreign worker id rejects the whole batch.
  - Workers who aren't eligible, or who already hold an offer, are skipped
    with a reason.

Rules:
  - Offers reserve no headcount.
  - One live offer per worker and shift.
  - No ranking: candidates are alphabetical.

Capabilities: offers reuse assignment.manage; no new capability was added.

Document: docs/architecture/SHIFT_OFFER_MODEL.md.

======================================================================
13. OFFER ACCEPTANCE / CONCURRENCY
======================================================================
Acceptance order:
  1. Share lock on the relationship.
  2. Lock the shift row.
  3. Lock the offer: CHO09 if it isn't open, CHO10 if it has expired
     (checked server-side, deterministic even before the expiry job
     runs).
  4. Run internal.perform_assignment. This is the same core that direct
     assignment uses (direct assignment was refactored onto it). It
     re-runs compliance, schedule and capacity and records a decision with
     the worker as actor.
  5. Create the assignment only if everything passes. It is created as
     accepted and attributed to the scheduler who made the offer.

When the shift fills, the remaining live offers close as shift_filled.

A lock-order review found a potential deadlock between relationship changes
and assignment and opening. It was fixed with a single global order:
relationship → shift → person → offer. open_shift and
accept_shift_assignment were redefined to follow it.

Tests:
  - Integration: two workers race for the last slot; exactly one wins and
    the other offer closes.
  - pgTAP I: a live offer on a full shift is refused SHIFT_FULL and closed.

17. STRUCTURAL INTEGRITY
======================================================================
Offers — composite foreign keys ensure that:
  - the offer belongs to the shift's agency;
  - the worker is in the same agency;
  - the person matches the worker (profile);
  - the actor is a member of the agency;
  - an accepted offer points to an assignment in the same agency.
Other offer rules:
  - one live offer per worker and shift (unique index);
  - expiry after offering;
  - ownership columns and expiry are immutable;
  - terminal states are immutable.

Issues: tied to the assignment and agency by composite foreign key; the
assignment must belong to the stated shift (trigger).

Outbox: audience organisation is required; recipient is required (legacy
rows excepted).

Every one of these was tested as the database owner.

23. SECURITY FINDINGS
======================================================================
Reviewed areas, each with its disposition:
  - Notification cross-tenant leakage: the audience organisation is
    checked; the address comes from active membership; the template
    verifies the subject's tenant. Tested.
  - Recipient leakage: no addresses stored or audited; projections show
    names only within the tenant. Tested.
  - Template injection: every value escaped; no control characters possible
    in subjects. Tested.
  - Raw token leakage: no tokens, signed URLs or one-click links.
  - Duplicate delivery: SKIP LOCKED, lease, token, idempotency key.
    Tested.
  - Provider retry abuse: bounded retries, then failed. Tested.
  - Concurrent outbox processing: exclusive claims. Tested.
  - Offer IDOR and acceptance spoofing: CHO04. Tested.
  - Stale eligibility on acceptance: the full gate is re-run. Tested.
  - Concurrent acceptance or overfill: row lock plus trigger. Tested.
  - Relationship suspension race: share/update locks; deadlock order fixed.
  - Issue tampering: no write grants; delete refused.
  - Facility visibility creep: no offer or issue path. Tested.
  - Service-role exposure: none used; the bundle scan is clean.

FIXED during the stage:
  - MEDIUM: a column-name shadowing bug made the batch tenancy check in
    offer_shift_to_workers ineffective. A foreign id raised a generic
    CH404 later instead of rejecting the batch. Fixed and tested.
  - MEDIUM: a potential deadlock between relationship changes and
    assignment, opening and acceptance, from inconsistent lock order.
    Fixed with the global order.
  - LOW: accepting an offer produced an `assigned` assignment that needed a
    second acceptance. It is now `accepted`.

ACCEPTED risks:
  - MEDIUM (bounded by secret handling): anyone holding the dispatcher
    database credential could claim due notifications. That exposes
    recipient addresses and shift display data across tenants, and allows
    suppressing delivery. Mitigations:
      * the credential is server-only and never NEXT_PUBLIC (bundle scan);
      * the role can only claim and complete;
      * the password is per environment and rotatable.
  - LOW: the email banner PNG is 1.6 MB. That hurts deliverability and
    load time; see Known Limitations.

There are no HIGH or CRITICAL findings.

24. KNOWN LIMITATIONS
======================================================================
  - Delivery needs the operator configuration in §28. Until it is done,
    rows accumulate as pending; nothing is lost and nothing is sent late
    beyond deliver_until.
  - Resend's idempotency window is 24 h. A manual replay after that could
    duplicate.
  - No notification preferences: all events are required.
  - Recipients cap at 25 operations users per event.
  - Readiness is re-evaluated hourly, not the moment a credential changes.
    A worker's acceptance always re-checks live.
  - The candidate scan is bounded at 2000 workers per request.
  - The dispatcher processes a batch sequentially; the default is 25 per
    minute. Raise the batch size or cadence for volume.
  - public/email/chelth-email-banner.png is 1.6 MB. Recommend a compressed
    version under ~150 KB before production sends.
  - format:check reports two untracked files that I did not create:
    public/brand/chelth/BRAND-ASSETS.md and src/styles/brand.css. They are
    in-progress brand work, left untouched; `npx prettier --write` on them
    will clear the check. Every tracked and stage file passes.
