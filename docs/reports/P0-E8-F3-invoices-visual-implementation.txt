3. INVOICE OVERVIEW CHANGES
---------------------------
Built as the Payroll sibling: same structure, same primitives.

- Header:
  - PageHeader reference "Invoices", with the subtitle "Prepare, review and
    export facility invoice drafts from approved, priced work. Chelth does
    not send invoices or collect payment."
  - Meta: an "Internal drafts — not sent" chip and "Bill side only · no tax
    calculated".
  - "Prepare Invoice Draft" CTA (invoice.prepare, when weeks are ready).
- StepUpNotice: unchanged wording and behaviour.
- KPI cards (region "Invoice summary"; real counts over the loaded
  records):
  - Needs Attention: invoice issues; footer "N adjustments required";
    anchors to its panel.
  - Ready to Draft: billable weeks not in a draft; footer "N facilities";
    anchors to its panel.
  - Awaiting Approval: reviewed drafts; footer "N draft · N ready to lock";
    quick filter status=reviewed.
  - Ready to Export: locked drafts; footer "N exported"; quick filter
    status=locked.
- Filter row: LockedFilterSelect "Facility" (from the drafts) and "Draft
  status", with "Show" and "Clear filters" (form "Filter invoice drafts").
- Invoice Drafts (RefPanel, REF_TABLE, region "Invoice drafts table"):
  - Reference (with export count);
  - Facility (pin);
  - Week;
  - Lines;
  - Billed Time;
  - Bill Total (right-aligned, formatMoney from integer minor units);
  - Status chip plus attention chip;
  - "⋯" opens the drawer.
  The row is mint with a 3 px teal inset while its drawer is open.
- Ready to Draft (region "Billable invoice work"):
  - Facility, Week (currency, relationship status), Workers, Time, Bill
    Total.
  - The existing "Create draft" form, or an "Adjustment required" chip.
  - This replaces the per-facility sub-tables with one table that has a
    Facility column.
- Needs Attention: the existing IssuesTable ("Invoice issues").
- Invoice Adjustments: a "Bill-side delta only" chip and an explanatory
  note.
  - Shows the existing candidates and adjustments tables.
  - When both are empty, the locked quiet tile: "No adjustments required."
- Reconciliation: the existing ReconciliationTable with a note that this
  is revision reconciliation, not payment tracking.
- Empty states use the locked quiet tile, for example "No invoice drafts
  yet. Approved work appears here when it is ready for invoice
  preparation."


4. DRAWER IMPLEMENTATION (Invoice Details)
------------------------------------------
- Canonical profile drawer, as Payroll, Workforce and Facility.
  - Title "Invoice Details"; trigger "Invoice details for <reference>".
- Identity: luminous invoice tile (84 px), reference (Manrope 21 / 700),
  facility (pin), week, lifecycle chip.
- Overview tab:
  - Draft Summary: bill total, currency, lines, billed time, status,
    prepared by, prepared at, documents.
  - Approval Control: maker-checker Required / Not required, the attention
    chip, and an internal-draft note.
- Lines tab: the real billed lines (worker, date, discipline, time, bill
  amount). Bill rates are not repeated here; they are on the record.
- History tab: the real draft history (HistoryList), newest first.
- Details are loaded for the first 15 listed drafts. Further rows say the
  details are on the record.
- Footer (lifecycle- and capability-aware):
  - The primary CTA is the next step this viewer can take: Review Draft
    (draft + invoice.prepare), Approve Draft (reviewed + invoice.approve,
    not blocked), Lock Draft (approved + invoice.approve, not blocked) or
    Export Draft Documents (locked + invoice.export). Each opens the
    record's Next Step section.
  - Otherwise the primary CTA is "Open Invoice Draft".
- No mutation runs in the drawer. Bill side only: no pay rate, pay amount
  or margin (asserted).


5. RECORD-PAGE IMPLEMENTATION (invoice draft)
---------------------------------------------
- RecordPage; PageHeader reference; back link "Invoices"; title =
  reference; subtitle "facility · week · currency".
- Meta: lifecycle chip, attention chip, "Draft invoice — internal, not
  sent", and "No tax calculated · not a request for payment".
- Section tabs: Summary · Next Step · Documents · Lines · History (all
  real; Next Step is hidden when voided).
- Notices: StepUpNotice (unchanged); LockedNotice for "Adjustment
  required" and "Includes revised work" (role status / alert kept).
- Summary:
  - Draft totals: bill total before any tax, lines, billed time, facility,
    billing week, currency.
  - Draft lifecycle: prepared, reviewed, approved and locked (time and
    actor); exported.
  - Immutability note: bill amounts are copied from pricing, a locked draft
    never changes, and corrections are separate adjustments.
- Next Step:
  - Maker-checker notice when it is on (draft / reviewed).
  - The existing forms with unchanged labels and gates: Mark reviewed,
    Approve draft, Lock draft, Export draft PDF / CSV, Void.
  - Lock note.
- Draft Documents: the existing ExportsTable plus a note: deterministic
  PDF / CSV, SHA-256 checksum, private storage, recorded downloads, not
  sent.
- Lines: the existing table (bill rate and amount, source revision).
- History: unchanged.


6. ADJUSTMENT-PAGE IMPLEMENTATION (invoice adjustment)
------------------------------------------------------
- Same arrangement as the Payroll adjustment record.
  - Title INV-ADJ-…; subtitle "direction · facility · week · currency".
  - Meta: lifecycle chip, attention chip (revision state), "Draft invoice
    adjustment — internal, not sent", and "Bill-side delta · the original
    draft never changes · no tax".
- Section tabs: What It Adjusts · Net Change · Next Step · Documents ·
  Changed Lines · History.
- What It Adjusts ("Adjustment lineage"): original draft link with
  "(unchanged)", Follows (when chained), revision change, direction.
- Net Change ("Net bill adjustment"):
  - signed net with its direction text (SignedAmount);
  - additional charges, credits, billed time;
  - a note that the original keeps its amounts.
- Next Step: the existing forms and maker-checker logic (preparedByMe) are
  unchanged; the LockedNotice reads "You prepared this adjustment…".
- Changed Lines: original → revised bill time, rate and amount, plus the
  signed change.
- No pay-side field exists anywhere on the page (asserted: no "pay rate",
  "margin" or pay delta).


7. LIFECYCLE STATES PRESERVED
-----------------------------
Draft → Reviewed → Approved → Locked → Exported, and Voided. The existing
INVOICE_DRAFT_STATUS_LABELS are used.

Tones match the existing InvoiceStatusBadge:
- Draft and Voided: neutral.
- Reviewed, Approved and Locked: info.
- Exported: success.

Attention / revision states use the existing labels:
- Adjustment required: amber.
- Adjustment in progress: blue.
- Revision resolved: neutral.
- Includes revised work: red.

Reconciliation keeps the existing vocabulary, including "Adjustment
required", "Adjustment in progress" and "Adjusted or no financial
change".

- Search; date-range picker; bulk selection.

11. SECURITY / CAPABILITY BEHAVIOUR
-----------------------------------
- Every action is rendered only behind the existing checks:
  - invoice.prepare: create, review, void (draft / reviewed);
  - invoice.approve: approve, lock, void (approved), not blocked;
  - invoice.export: PDF / CSV and downloads.
- AAL2: the StepUpNotice is unchanged on the overview and records. The
  database still returns MFA_REQUIRED.
- Maker-checker: unchanged.
  - Adjustments hide Approve for the preparer (existing preparedByMe).
  - Drafts show an informational notice when the setting is on, and the
    database refuses the preparer (MAKER_CHECKER, audited). The draft
    model has no "prepared by me" field, as with payroll batches.
- The drawer runs no mutation. No service role and no client-only
  authorization. New reads (draft lines and history for the drawer) use
  the existing user-scoped RPCs the record already uses.

15. KNOWN GAPS
--------------
- Finance shell at 412 / 375: the tab row starts scrolled to the left, so
  the active "Invoices" tab (the last one) is only partly visible until the
  row is scrolled. The shell is locked in this phase, so this was not
  changed. A follow-up could scroll the active tab into view in
  FinanceWorkspaceNav.
- Draft maker-checker: the draft model has no "prepared by me" field, so
  Approve stays visible to the preparer and the database refuses it,
  audited. This is the same as payroll batches.
- Filters cover facility and status over the loaded drafts only; there is
  no search or date range.
- The drawer loads lines and history for the first 15 listed drafts.
- The shared tables (issues, adjustments, reconciliation, exports) keep
  their bordered DataTable look inside locked panels. They are shared with
  Payroll and were not restyled.
