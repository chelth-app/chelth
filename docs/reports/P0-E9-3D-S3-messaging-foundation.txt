2. DATA MODEL (S3.2 / S3.3)
---------------------------
Migrations:
- 20261010110000_messaging_vocabulary.sql
- 20261010110100_operational_messaging.sql

New enums:
- public.conversation_kind: worker | facility.
- public.message_sender_side: worker | agency | facility.
- internal.notification_event: + message_received.

public.conversation_threads:
- id, agency_organisation_id, kind;
- agency_worker_id and worker_profile_id (worker threads);
- relationship_id (facility threads);
- shift_id (optional context: a shift or a staffing request);
- created_by_profile_id, created_at, last_message_at.

Constraints:
- Composite FKs bind the worker / relationship / shift to the same agency.
- A check enforces the kind's shape: workers are never on facility
  threads.
- Unique (NULLS NOT DISTINCT): one worker thread per (worker, shift or
  general); one facility thread per (relationship, shift or general).

public.messages:
- id, thread_id, agency_organisation_id;
- sender_profile_id (no FK, like audit_events: history outlives
  identities);
- sender_side (decided by the server);
- body: text, 1–2000 characters, newlines only;
- client_key;
- created_at = clock_timestamp() (server time at insert).
- unique (thread_id, sender_profile_id, client_key).

public.message_receipts: (thread_id, profile_id) → last_read_at,
last_read_message_id.

Conceptual contexts (S3.3):
- WORKER_AGENCY = worker thread without a shift;
- SHIFT = worker thread with shift_id;
- FACILITY_CONTEXT = facility thread without a shift;
- STAFFING_REQUEST = facility thread with shift_id.

The UI is not coupled to enum labels; it renders from the context columns.


3. IMMUTABILITY AND RETENTION (S3.4 / S3.5)
-------------------------------------------
- Append-only. A trigger refuses UPDATE / DELETE on messages and DELETE on
  threads (CH409), even for the owner role.
- No editing in v1, and no Edited marker needed.
- History is never deleted, including when:
  - a shift completes or a request closes;
  - a relationship ends;
  - a worker leaves or is suspended (they lose access; the record stays);
  - a staff member loses a role.
- A thread becoming inaccessible to one side never removes it from the
  other.
- No retention purge is implemented, so retention is indefinite. A future
  retention policy must be an explicit, audited operator process.
- No HIPAA claim is made on the strength of this feature.

5. AUTHORISATION / RLS (S3.7)
-----------------------------
Deny by default. authz.thread_side(thread, capability) derives the caller's
side on every read and write:
- worker: the thread's worker, while their membership and worker record are
  active;
- agency: message.view / message.send in the agency;
- facility: the same capabilities in the explicitly linked facility
  organisation for the thread's relationship (the existing
  has_relationship_capability: relationship not ended, record linked).

Rules:
- Workers are never in facility threads; facility users never see worker
  threads.
- Worker ↔ facility direct messaging is not provided (S1 D5).
- RLS: SELECT on threads and messages via authz.can_read_thread; on
  receipts only one's own.
- No INSERT / UPDATE / DELETE grants or policies: writes only through
  security-definer RPCs with a pinned search_path.
- Capabilities (non-privileged; no AAL2 needed to message):
  - message.view and message.send: agency admin, operations manager,
    scheduler, recruiter, credentialing officer; facility admin and
    scheduler;
  - none: finance, facility supervisor;
  - workers need no grant.
- Revocation is automatic. Losing a membership, worker status, capability
  or relationship removes access immediately, with no clean-up (tested:
  a suspended worker reads 0).

RPCs:
- open_worker_thread(worker, shift?): the worker themself or staff with
  message.send. The shift must be one the worker is or was assigned to.
  Get-or-create under an advisory lock. Audited (message.thread_opened, no
  content).
- open_facility_thread(relationship, shift?): agency or linked facility
  with message.send. The shift must be under that relationship and not a
  draft.
- send_message(thread, body, client_key): sender side, validation,
  idempotency, rate limit (300 / day / person), last_message_at, the
  sender's own receipt, and the notification.
- mark_thread_read(thread).
- list_my_threads(organisation, unread_only?, limit?): an agency, or a
  facility organisation for its relationship threads.
- get_thread(thread): returns no row when missing or hidden (no oracle);
  includes can_send.
- list_thread_messages(thread, before_at?, before_id?, limit?): keyset
  pagination, newest first.
- unread_message_count(organisation).

Sender labels:
- your own messages show "You";
- workers are named to agency staff;
- same-side colleagues by name;
- the OTHER organisation only by its organisation name. Staff personal
  names never cross organisations.
- The worker's name is shown only to agency staff.


6. READ STATE (S3.8)
--------------------
- The unread count per thread is messages from others newer than your
  last_read_at.
- unread_message_count gives the total; mark_thread_read records the
  latest message.
- Sending marks your own message read.
- No typing indicators, presence or delivery receipts.


7. DUPLICATE SEND / IDEMPOTENCY (S3.9)
--------------------------------------
- The browser generates one client key per composed message.
- A double tap, a mobile retry or a resubmission with the same key returns
  the original message (duplicate = true).
- Enforced by a unique constraint and ON CONFLICT, safe under concurrency.


8. NOTIFICATIONS (S3.10)
------------------------
- When the agency side posts in a worker thread, one message_received
  notice is queued for the worker.
- The existing "one open notice per (event, subject, recipient)" rule
  coalesces bursts per thread.
- internal.notification_template has a conversation_thread branch:
  - worker audience only;
  - agency and facility name and shift times when present;
  - path /app/organisations/<agency>/messages/<thread>.
- Never the message body.
- E-mail: "You have a new Chelth message regarding your shift" — "<Agency>
  sent you a message … the message itself is not included in this e-mail."
- Staff are not e-mailed per message in v1 (in-app unread counts instead).
  This avoids mailing every capability holder; per-staff routing is a
  later decision.
- Push notifications are deferred: no push infrastructure exists.
