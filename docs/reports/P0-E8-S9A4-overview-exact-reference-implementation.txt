A. QA DATA AT REFERENCE DENSITY (fixture only)
----------------------------------------------
New spec: tests/e2e/overview-fidelity.spec.ts.

It builds its own world through the existing public RPCs only:
- create_agency_facility, create_facility_location,
  create_facility_relationship and set_facility_relationship_status;
- create_shift, assign_worker_to_shift, accept_shift_assignment,
  clock_in_assignment;
- submit_facility_shift_request and open_shift.

There is no seed, migration or product change.

What it creates:
- Today's Schedule: 5 accepted day-shift assignments across 3 client
  facilities, with 2 workers clocked in.
- Staffing Requests: 5 facility requests over the coming days, 2 of them
  opened by the agency.

Day shifts in every run: the 3 facilities are created in whichever real
IANA timezone currently has local midday, so the schedule shows ordinary
day shifts (7:00 AM – 3:00 PM …) whenever the suite runs. In the US evening
the New York fixture facility would otherwise show overnight shifts.

The spec asserts 5 schedule rows, 5 request rows and populated workforce
counts. It captures 1512 x 996 (the reference frame), 1536 x 1024, 1280 and
412 from the production build (next start).

  icons and the chart line. The implementation uses the brand semantic

  existing line icon set at a heavier stroke. Introducing a new icon family
  is not allowed.

  the one linked facility (Mercy Rehab).

  reference "Requested" timestamp.

Type

  Manrope weight.

  The reference renders a narrower face at a larger size.

Logo
- The canonical asset's own aspect makes its box taller (the width
  matches).

==========================================================================
LOGO FIDELITY — DESCRIPTOR LEGIBILITY FINDING
==========================================================================

The sidebar uses the canonical vector asset /public/brand/chelth/logo-reverse.svg
directly. It is unmodified, with a 56 px height, the canonical aspect, and a soft
aqua halo on the mark.

Finding
- In the canonical SVG, the descriptor "HEALTHCARE WORKFORCE OPERATIONS"
  is white outlined glyphs about 13 units tall in a 264-unit lockup. At
  56 px that is about a 2.8 px cap height. It is already pure white, so no
  contrast headroom remains.
- On retina displays (2x) the unmodified asset renders crisp and legible.
- On standard-density displays (1x) it is legible but faint.
- The locked PNG's logo artwork sets the descriptor larger and heavier
  relative to the wordmark (about 110 px wide against about 95 px in the
  canonical file). The reference artwork and the production asset differ in
  this detail.

Tried and rejected
- A second copy of the canonical asset, clipped to the descriptor band,
  with hairline faux-bold (zero-blur white shadows). At both 1x and 2x it
  double-struck and clotted the letterforms ("OPERATIIONS"). That is worse
  than the original and effectively alters the mark, so it was reverted.

Evidence: docs/reports/overview-fidelity/logo/01-logo-reference-1x-2x-comparison.png

Decision needed (brand owner)
- Matching the reference descriptor at 1x needs a brand-approved asset
  change: a reverse lockup with the descriptor set larger or heavier (a
  small-size / UI variant).
- Once approved and placed in /public/brand/chelth/, it is a one-line swap
  in BrandLogo. No asset was edited in this pass.
