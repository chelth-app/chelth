6. PAYROLL LIFECYCLE MAPPING
----------------------------
Draft → Reviewed → Approved → Locked → Exported; Cancelled before lock.
The existing domain labels (PAYROLL_BATCH_STATUS_LABELS) are used.

Tones (unchanged meaning):
- Draft and Cancelled: neutral.
- Reviewed, Approved and Locked: info (in progress).
- Exported: success.

Attention tones:
- Adjustment required: amber.
- Includes revised work: red.
- Adjustment in progress: blue.
- Revision resolved: neutral.

KPI cards (real counts over the loaded records):
- Needs Attention: payroll issues; footer "N adjustments required"; anchors
  to Needs Attention.
- Ready to Prepare: priced periods not in a batch; footer "N workers";
  anchors to Ready to Prepare.
- Awaiting Approval: reviewed batches; footer "N draft · N ready to lock";
  quick filter status=reviewed.
- Ready to Export: locked, not yet exported; footer "N exported"; quick
  filter status=locked.

No Paid, Outstanding, Funds or Tax KPIs exist.

Language:
- "Prepare Payroll Batch" (header CTA), "Review Batch", "Approve Batch",
  "Lock Batch", "Export CSV".
- "Preparation and export only" chip; "Chelth does not pay workers."


7. DRAWER IMPLEMENTATION (Payroll Batch Details)
------------------------------------------------
- Trigger: "⋯" per row, named "Payroll batch details for <reference>".
- Identity: payroll tile, reference, "Payroll batch · <currency>", period
  and lifecycle chip.
- Overview tab:
  - Batch Summary: pay total, currency, workers, lines, status, prepared by,
    prepared at, exports.
  - Approval Control: maker-checker Required / Not required (plus "by an
    eligible member other than the preparer" when required), the attention
    chip and the preparation-only line.
- Workers tab: real worker totals (lines, time, overtime, pay).
- History tab: real batch history (HistoryList), newest first.
- Details are loaded for the first 15 listed batches. Further rows say the
  details are on the record.
- Footer (lifecycle- and capability-aware):
  - The primary CTA is the next step this viewer can take: Review Batch
    (draft + payroll.prepare), Approve Batch (reviewed + payroll.approve,
    not blocked), Lock Batch (approved + payroll.approve, not blocked) or
    Export CSV (locked + payroll.export). Each opens the record's Next Step
    section.
  - Otherwise the primary CTA is "Open Payroll Batch".
  - No mutation runs in the drawer. The existing forms on the record keep
    step-up, blocking and maker-checker handling, so the drawer cannot show
    an impossible action.
- Native modal dialog: focus moves into the drawer; Escape returns focus to
  the row trigger (tested).

9. ADJUSTMENT IMPLEMENTATION
----------------------------
Adjustments stay distinct from batch entries.

Overview: a "Payroll Adjustments" panel with a "Pay-side delta only" chip,
the delta explanation, and the existing candidates and adjustments tables:
- PAY-ADJ references;
- signed net change with Increase / Decrease text;
- "Revision 1 → 2" lineage.
When there are no candidates and no adjustments, the panel uses the locked
quiet tile instead of the shared dashed empty state. That fix is scoped to
Payroll, so Invoices is unaffected.

Adjustment record:
- RecordPage; title PAY-ADJ-…; subtitle worker · week · currency.
- Meta: lifecycle chip, attention chip, an "Adjustment — not payment" chip,
  and "Pay-side delta · the original batch never changes".
- Section tabs: What It Adjusts · Net Change · Next Step · Exports ·
  Changed Lines · History.
- Lineage, net change (SignedAmount), changed lines (original → revised)
  and the immutable history are unchanged in content.

No refund, repayment or clawback state was introduced.


10. MAKER / CHECKER TREATMENT
-----------------------------
The logic is unchanged.

The database enforces it for batches and adjustments: financial_authorize
returns MAKER_CHECKER when the preparer approves and the setting is on, and
the denial is audited.

Surfaced in the UI:
- Batch drawer, Approval Control: "Maker-checker: Required / Not
  required".
- Batch record, while draft or reviewed with maker-checker on: an info
  notice, "Second approver required — Prepared by <name>. Approval must be
  completed by another eligible member."
  The batch model exposes no "prepared by me" flag, so the existing Approve
  button is shown as before and the database refuses the preparer.
- Adjustment record (existing preparedByMe logic, unchanged): an info
  notice, "Second approver required — You prepared this adjustment. A
  different finance member must approve it." Approve stays hidden for the
  preparer, as before.
- When maker-checker is off: no warning noise; only "Not required" in the
  drawer.


11. EXPORT TREATMENT
