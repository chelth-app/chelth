1. E8 OBJECTIVE
---------------
Bring every Chelth surface onto one approved, locked visual system: the
same shell, typography, palette, surfaces, chips, icons, drawers, records
and CTA hierarchy. The result must be premium, calm, healthcare-native,
operational and trusted, and must change NO product behaviour, data access
or security boundary.

Each family was implemented from its canonical reference in
docs/ui-reference/p08-e8/canonical/, approved by human visual review, and
then held by the locked system (docs/ui-reference/CHELTH-LOCKED-VISUAL-
SYSTEM.md).
