CHELTH — P0-E6-S2 TIMESHEETS & ATTENDANCE REVIEW HARDENING
Stage report · 2026-09-30 · branch p0-e6-s2-timesheets (uncommitted)

======================================================================
1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
======================================================================
Chelth now turns attendance into weekly timesheets that can be reviewed,
signed off and locked. Timesheets never become a second clock.

  - Source of truth. Attendance events stay the only source of worked
    time:
      * device events carry server time;
      * breaks are events too;
      * corrections and reviewer adjustments add new corrected events.
    One database function, internal.effective_time, computes effective
    times, breaks and worked minutes. The attendance projection, My Shifts
    and every timesheet entry use it.
  - Derived timesheets.
      * One timesheet per worker per week; one entry per assignment.
      * Entries are written only by the trusted derivation, which runs on
        every attendance or assignment change.
      * Nobody can write minutes. The API has no write grant, and a
        trigger refuses writes outside the trusted path, even for the
        table owner.
  - Lifecycle. Worker submits (reasons are returned when blocked); the
    agency approves, which stores an immutable snapshot; each linked
    facility signs off its own entries or raises a discrepancy; the last
    sign-off locks the timesheet.
  - Revisions. Changing attendance behind an approved or locked timesheet
    needs:
      * an explicit confirmation from the reviewer;
      * the timesheet.approve capability.
    It then:
      * supersedes the approval and the facility decisions;
      * increments the revision;
      * requires re-approval and fresh facility sign-off.
    Nothing historical is edited.
  - Attendance review hardening:
      * breaks, with an on_break state;
      * reviewer-adjusted approvals (a different time with a structured
        reason and a note);
      * reviewer-originated adjustments;
      * "not worked" closure for no-shows;
      * a full correction history page;
      * a raw evidence viewer behind MFA step-up.
  - Closed the P0-E6-S1 findings:
      * F5: location evidence retention (default 90 days), a daily bounded
        purge that removes coordinates only, and a narrow legal hold;
      * F6: refused clock-in rate limit;
      * F7: facility-view audit deduplicated per viewer.
  - Validation. All 13 checks pass:
      * pgTAP: 22 files, 1,007 assertions, all 26 required cases (A–Z);
      * integration: 84 tests;
      * unit: 213 tests;
      * E2E: flows 1–7 on desktop and mobile (see §26 and §32).
  - Security. No open HIGH or CRITICAL finding.
  - Out of scope, as instructed: pay rates, bill rates, overtime, payroll,
    exports, invoices, accounting, tax, reimbursement, expenses, automated
    approval or billing, and GPS beyond the existing point-in-time
    evidence.

A second local Claude session (chelth-cc) was working in the same tree and
on the same local Supabase stack. Its stack restarts caused flaky runs; we
coordinated, and it paused. It contributed one reviewed change: facility
self sign-off is refused (CH403). The migration and the pgTAP test are
included in the counts above.

2. TIMESHEET SCHEMA
======================================================================
Migration 20260930150200_timesheet_tables.sql:

  agency_timesheet_settings
    week_starts_on (ISO 1–7, default Monday).
  timesheets
    Agency + worker + person + period, with period_end = start + 6.
      - status, revision;
      - submission, approval, lock and rejection fields;
      - unique per (worker, period).
  timesheet_entries
    One per assignment.
      - scheduled start and end, local date, timezone;
      - derived values: effective start and end, breaks (jsonb), break and
        worked minutes, not_worked, complete, blocking_reasons,
        open_exception_types, pending_corrections;
      - calculation_version, revision, facility_state,
        facility_organisation_id.
  timesheet_approvals
    Immutable snapshot and totals per revision. Supersede-only.
  timesheet_facility_signoffs
    Per-entry decision with a snapshot, dispute reason and note, plus
    resolution and supersede fields. Otherwise immutable.
  timesheet_history
    Append-only lifecycle log.

None of these tables has a pay, bill, rate or currency column.

======================================================================
3. TIMESHEET PERIOD MODEL
======================================================================
  - Weekly periods only. Payroll calendars are deferred.
  - internal.period_start_for(org, local_date) is deterministic. An entry
    belongs to the week of its shift's LOCAL start date, so overnight
    shifts stay in their start week.
  - The week start is fixed once an agency has timesheets (CHP15).
  - Timesheets are created lazily when an assignment is accepted. No
    period-creation job exists.
  - A week can be submitted when its last local date has ended everywhere
    (period_end + 1 day at 12:00 UTC, which is after midnight in UTC−12).

======================================================================
4. ENTRY DERIVATION
======================================================================
internal.sync_timesheet_entry(assignment) is the only writer of entries.

When it runs:
  - from internal.refresh_attendance, i.e. after every clock, break,
    correction, adjustment, exception or scan change;
  - from an AFTER trigger on shift_assignments (insert, or status change);
  - through internal.rebuild_timesheet, and the agency-facing
    rebuild_timesheet (idempotent, never destructive).

What it does:
  - An included entry is one with an effective clock-in, or an accepted
    assignment on a shift that is not cancelled.
  - Other entries stay in place with included = false. Nothing is
    deleted.
  - When a derived time changes on an approved or locked timesheet, it
    creates a revision (§11).
  - Only exception metadata may change on approved rows.

Protection:
  - authenticated has SELECT only;
  - trigger timesheet_entries_guard refuses writes unless the
    transaction-local flag is set by internal functions;
  - time columns are frozen while the timesheet is approved or locked
    (CHP09).

======================================================================
5. ATTENDANCE SEGMENT / BREAK MODEL
======================================================================
  - New events: break_start, break_end, corrected_break_start and
    corrected_break_end. A break event's segment is its break number
    (1–20); clock events are constrained to segment 1.
  - Unique indexes allow one device start and one device end per break.
  - States: not_started → clocked_in ⇄ on_break → clocked_out.
  - start_break_assignment and end_break_assignment:
      * own assignment only, server time, no location;
      * CHT19 if already on a break, CHT20 if not on a break;
      * clock-out while on a break is refused with CHT21.
  - No jurisdictional break rules are automated.

======================================================================
6. EFFECTIVE TIME CALCULATION
======================================================================
internal.effective_time(attendance):

  - Effective time for each (type, segment): the latest corrected event,
    else the device event.
  - worked = whole_minutes(in, out) − Σ break minutes, where
    whole_minutes(a, b) = floor(epoch(b)/60) − floor(epoch(a)/60).
  - Minutes are computed on UTC instants, so DST and odd offsets cannot
    skew them.
  - Reasons:
      * MISSING_CLOCK_IN, MISSING_CLOCK_OUT: no minutes, and an end time is
        never invented;
      * BREAK_NOT_ENDED;
      * TIMES_INCONSISTENT.
  - "Not worked" gives a complete zero-minute entry.
  - Scheduled duration is never used.
  - Calculation version is 1. Payroll rounding is deferred.

======================================================================
7. WORKER SUBMISSION
======================================================================
submit_timesheet:

  - Own timesheet only, from open or rejected.
  - Rebuilds, then checks the blocking codes:
      * PERIOD_NOT_ENDED
      * NO_WORK
      * MISSING_CLOCK_IN
      * MISSING_CLOCK_OUT
      * BREAK_NOT_ENDED
      * TIMES_INCONSISTENT
      * PENDING_CORRECTION
  - Blocked: returns outcome 'blocked' plus the codes, and writes
    nothing.
  - Success: history entry, audit event, and a notice to agency
    approvers.
  - The UI lists each reason with guidance on what to do.

======================================================================
8. AGENCY APPROVAL
======================================================================
approve_timesheet(timesheet, expected_revision):

  - Requires timesheet.approve.
  - Never the worker themself (CH403). A worker without timesheet.view
    gets CHP04.
  - The expected revision must still match (CHP14).
  - Rebuilds, then checks the submission codes plus UNREVIEWED_EXCEPTION:
    every attendance exception must be acknowledged or dismissed first.
  - Writes an immutable snapshot:
      * derived times, breaks, minutes, not_worked;
      * exception types, approved correction ids;
      * totals and calculation version;
      * no coordinates or notes.
  - Entries at linked facilities (relationship not ended) become pending.
    If none are pending, the timesheet locks immediately.
  - reject_timesheet(reason, note) returns the timesheet to the worker.

Reviewers never edit hours. The review page shows:
  - scheduled times, effective clock-in and clock-out, and breaks;
  - worked time, exceptions and approved corrections;
  - facility and location result;
  - a link to each entry's full attendance history.

======================================================================
9. FACILITY SIGN-OFF
======================================================================
facility_decide_timesheet_entry(entry, expected_revision, sign_off,
reason, note):

  - Scope: timesheet.facility_signoff through the entry's relationship,
    plus membership of the entry's facility organisation.
  - Only on agency-approved timesheets, and only for pending entries.
  - The entry's own worker cannot sign it off (CH403; the peer session's
    change).
  - The last sign-off locks the timesheet.

What the facility sees (list_facility_timesheet_entries; access is audited
once per 15 minutes):
  - worker name, agency, facility and location;
  - date and scheduled shift;
  - effective start and end, breaks, worked minutes;
  - "reviewed by agency" indicator and sign-off state.

Never shown to the facility: coordinates, notes, credentials, other
facilities, other agencies' work.

======================================================================
10. MULTI-FACILITY HANDLING
======================================================================
  - Sign-off is per entry. A week spanning Mercy (Gamma) and Riverside
    (Delta) needs both.
  - Gamma sees and signs only Mercy; Delta only Riverside.
  - Cross-signing fails with CHP12. Listing another facility's entries
    fails with CH403.
  - One notice per facility organisation per approval.
  - An unlinked facility's entries need no sign-off.
  - Tested in pgTAP (C, D), in integration, and in E2E Flow 3.

======================================================================
11. REVISION MODEL
======================================================================
internal.revise_timesheet:
  - supersedes the approval and the live facility decisions;
  - resolves open disputes as timesheet_revised;
  - increments the revision;
  - sets the status to 'submitted' (automatic) or 'open' (reopen);
  - resets entry facility states;
  - writes history and audit.

A trigger only allows a revision increment when leaving approved or
locked.

  - Correction or adjustment on a locked timesheet: CHT22 unless
    confirmed and the reviewer holds timesheet.approve.
  - reopen_timesheet(reason, note): explicit; returns to the worker.
  - Every approval is kept, one per revision.

======================================================================
12. REVIEWER-ADJUSTED CORRECTIONS
======================================================================
review_attendance_correction gains four parameters:
  - approved_time;
  - adjustment_reason: facility_reported_time, supervisor_observation,
    device_or_app_problem, worker_statement, break_not_recorded or other;
  - note (≤ 500 characters);
  - confirm_revision.

Resolution approved_with_adjustment:
  - requires a DIFFERENT time and a reason;
  - the worker gets an attendance_time_adjusted notice;
  - no worker acknowledgment is required in this stage.

adjust_attendance_time: a reviewer-originated adjustment, stored as an
approved correction (origin reviewer_adjustment).

Common rules:
  - never on your own attendance;
  - consistency is re-checked by recalculation (CHT16), and a failure
    rolls everything back;
  - the original event always remains.

list_attendance_history returns original events → requests → decisions
(approved time, reasons, notes) → corrected events → the exception
lifecycle.
  - Agency: sees staff names.
  - Worker: sees their own history without staff names.

======================================================================
13. EXCEPTION GATING
======================================================================
  Submission (worker): MISSING_CLOCK_IN, MISSING_CLOCK_OUT,
    BREAK_NOT_ENDED, TIMES_INCONSISTENT, PENDING_CORRECTION,
    PERIOD_NOT_ENDED, NO_WORK.
  Approval (agency): all of the above, plus UNREVIEWED_EXCEPTION (any
    open or under-review exception).
  No-shows: a missed clock-in can be closed as not_worked (only when
    there is no effective clock-in), giving a complete zero-minute
    entry.

======================================================================
15. PURGE / LEGAL HOLD
======================================================================
Purge: internal.run_location_evidence_purge, pg_cron
chelth-location-evidence-purge, daily at 03:17 UTC.
  - Applies each agency's OWN retention period.
  - Batches of 1000, at most 20 batches, SKIP LOCKED.
  - Nulls coordinates and device time and sets purged_at. This is the
    only update the evidence table accepts, enforced by a trigger.
  - No deletes. The event and its result are untouched.
  - Idempotent.
  - Audited per agency as a count.

Legal hold: place and release (location.view, AAL2).
  - One active hold per record; holds are never deleted.
  - The reason is stored with the hold and never audited.
  - Held records are skipped by the purge.

======================================================================
16. CLOCK-IN RATE LIMIT
======================================================================
  - At most 10 committed refusals per worker + assignment per 10-minute
    window.
  - Beyond that: CH429, and the transaction rolls back. No exception,
    audit row or notification is written.
  - Successful clock-ins never consume the limit.
  - Tested: 10 audit rows, 1 exception and 1 notification after 11
    attempts; a legitimate clock-in from inside still works afterwards.

======================================================================
17. AUDIT VOLUME HARDENING
======================================================================
internal.record_audit_event_once writes one row per actor + action +
target per window, backed by the new index audit_events_dedupe_idx.
  - Facility attendance views: 15 minutes per viewer and shift.
  - Facility timesheet views: 15 minutes per viewer and facility.
  - Traceability is kept: every distinct viewer is still recorded.
  - Raw evidence reads are NOT deduplicated; every read is audited.

29. KNOWN LIMITATIONS
======================================================================
  - Weekly periods only. No early submission before the week has ended
    everywhere.
  - One work span per assignment. Multiple clock-in/out spans are not
    supported; breaks are.
  - Timesheet visibility for ended relationships: the facility loses the
    view once the relationship ends (the helper excludes ended
    relationships).
  - Worker acknowledgment of adjustments is not required. The worker is
    notified and sees the history.
  - Facilities cannot view decisions after lock beyond their own list.
    There is no export.
  - The agency list is capped at 500 rows, the worker list at 30 recent
    weeks, the facility list at 500.
  - Minute truncation is fixed (calculation v1). No rounding policy.
