CHELTH — P0-E7-S1 PAY & BILL RATE FOUNDATIONS
Stage report · 2026-10-01 · branch p0-e7-s1-pay-bill-rates (uncommitted)

======================================================================
1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
======================================================================
Chelth now has a pricing truth layer. Money consumes locked operational
evidence, and history never depends on current configuration.

  - Versioned rate cards. Each scope is agency + (one facility
    relationship, or all facilities) + discipline + (shift type, or any).
    Pay and bill rates are hourly, effective-dated and independent of each
    other. Each version has one ISO 4217 currency, and every amount is an
    integer in minor units.
  - Rate versions:
      * drafts can be edited;
      * activation freezes the terms;
      * a later version supersedes an earlier one only from its start date;
      * overlapping active periods are impossible (exclusion constraint).
  - One pricing engine prices a LOCKED timesheet revision from its
    immutable approval snapshot, with exact integer arithmetic. It applies:
      * rate precedence;
      * per-entry rounding (off by default);
      * weekly overtime per side (off by default).
    The result is an immutable record with one currency.
  - Missing, ambiguous or mixed-currency rates fail closed:
      * nothing is priced;
      * the timesheet appears in a "Needs attention" queue;
      * finance is notified once.
  - Repricing a revision is idempotent. A new locked revision gets a new
    record, and old records stay. Later rate changes never alter priced
    history: each line stores the applied version ids and the applied
    values.
  - Finance surfaces:
      * /rates (Current, Upcoming and Superseded versions, plus policies);
      * /pricing (Needs attention, Ready for pricing, Priced);
      * a priced timesheet detail page explaining every amount.
  - Least privilege:
      * rates.manage needs AAL2;
      * schedulers, facilities and workers see no pay, margin or pricing.
  - S7 from P0-E6-S2 is closed. Ending a relationship releases pending
    facility sign-offs, and a timesheet with nothing left pending locks.
    History and audit record the transition.
  - Validation: all 13 checks pass. Counts:
      * pgTAP: 24 files, 1,098 assertions (A–Z covered);
      * unit: 247 tests;
      * integration: 90 tests;
      * end-to-end: 110 passed, 2 skipped (desktop-only keyboard tests).
  - Security: no open HIGH or CRITICAL finding.

Not built, as instructed:
  - payroll, payslips, ACH or bank files;
  - tax, withholding, benefits or deductions;
  - reimbursements or expenses;
  - invoices or payment collection;
  - Stripe, QuickBooks, Xero, GL postings or exports;
  - automated payment or billing.

======================================================================
2. FINANCIAL DOMAIN BOUNDARY
======================================================================
Pricing tables are separate:
  - currencies;
  - rate_cards and rate_card_versions;
  - rounding_policy_versions and overtime_policy_versions;
  - priced_timesheets and priced_timesheet_lines;
  - pricing_blocks.

No pay or bill column was added to attendance_events, assignment_attendance,
shifts, shift_assignments or timesheet_entries.

Operational tables gained only:
  - shifts.classification: a controlled operational attribute used to
    select a rate;
  - composite unique keys that priced lines reference.

Pricing reads operational data; it never writes it.

======================================================================
3. RATE CARD SCHEMA
======================================================================
currencies
  Code (^[A-Z]{3}$), name, minor_unit_digits and active flag. Seeded:
  USD, CAD, GBP, EUR, AUD, NZD. Rows are immutable.

rate_cards
  Columns:
    - agency;
    - relationship_id (NULL = all facilities);
    - discipline_key (FK to disciplines);
    - classification (NULL = any);
    - created_by_membership_id.
  One card per scope (UNIQUE NULLS NOT DISTINCT). Cards are never updated
  or deleted.

rate_card_versions
  Columns:
    - card, version, status (draft | active | discarded);
    - currency;
    - pay_rate_minor and bill_rate_minor (bigint, 1 – 100,000,000);
    - effective_from, effective_to (inclusive), superseded_from;
    - generated effective_period;
    - created, activated and discarded by and at.
  Constraints:
    - EXCLUDE (card =, effective_period &&) WHERE active;
    - a transition trigger:
        * drafts are editable;
        * active versions are frozen except a one-time superseded_from;
        * discarded versions are frozen;
        * no delete or truncate.

======================================================================
4. RATE SCOPE & PRECEDENCE
======================================================================
Resolution uses the work's relationship, the shift's discipline and
classification, and the entry's local work date.

  1 relationship + discipline + classification
  2 relationship + discipline (any classification)
  3 all facilities + discipline + classification
  4 all facilities + discipline (any classification)

The first tier with a matching card decides:
  - If that card has no active version for the date:
    RATE_NOT_CONFIGURED. There is no fallback to a broader card.
  - If more than one match exists at a tier: RATE_AMBIGUOUS. The unique
    scope and the exclusion constraint already make this structurally
    impossible.

The applied tier is stored on every line. Worker names and free text are
never keys.

======================================================================
5. PAY VS BILL SEPARATION
======================================================================
Pay and bill are separate columns, entered separately and never derived
from each other. Each has:
  - its own overtime policy;
  - its own regular and overtime minute split;
  - its own amount.

Margin (bill − pay) is derived for finance display only and is never
stored.

======================================================================
6. MONEY REPRESENTATION
======================================================================
Integer minor units (bigint) throughout. For example, $42.50/h is 4250.

  - UI input is parsed exactly by concatenating digits (parseMajorToMinor);
    no floating point is involved.
  - Display builds an exact decimal string and formats it with the
    currency's own Intl.NumberFormat rules.
  - Zero and negative rates are rejected, so "missing" can never be
    confused with "free".

======================================================================
7. CURRENCY MODEL
======================================================================
One currency per version, enforced against active rows in currencies.
There is no FX.

A priced timesheet has exactly one currency, guaranteed in two ways:
  - priced lines carry the FK (priced_timesheet_id, currency);
  - mixed currencies across entries block pricing with
    RATE_CURRENCY_MISMATCH.

======================================================================
8. EFFECTIVE DATING
======================================================================
A version applies to [effective_from, min(effective_to + 1,
superseded_from)) on the entry's LOCAL work date.

  - Future versions can be scheduled.
  - Activating a version that starts inside an existing open-ended (or
    later-ending) version supersedes that version from the new start.
  - A version starting on or before an existing active version's start is
    refused (CHM12), as is one that would leave part of an existing
    version ambiguous. History is never rewritten.
  - The UI shows derived phases: Draft, Current, Upcoming, Superseded,
    Ended, Discarded.

======================================================================
9. RATE VERSIONING
======================================================================
Versions are numbered per card. Lifecycle: draft → active, or
draft → discarded.

  - Drafts are edited through update_rate_version_draft, which is
    audited as rate.updated_draft.
  - New terms require a new version.
  - All administration requires rates.manage at AAL2.
  - Audit actions:
      * rate.card_created, rate.created, rate.updated_draft;
      * rate.discarded, rate.activated, rate.superseded.
    Metadata carries ids, currency, minor-unit values and dates only.

======================================================================
10. SHIFT CLASSIFICATION
======================================================================
Enum values: regular | evening | night | weekend. Default: regular.

  - Existing and new shifts are regular unless set.
  - set_shift_classification (shift.manage) works while the shift is draft
    or submitted. A trigger then fixes the value once the shift is open.
  - It is shown on the shift page and editable before opening.
  - It is never inferred from labels.
  - Differential stacking is not implemented.

======================================================================
11. PRICING SNAPSHOT MODEL
======================================================================
priced_timesheets (unique per timesheet + revision)
  - approval FK (approval, timesheet, revision);
  - worker, period, currency;
  - totals (raw minutes, priced minutes, overtime minutes per side, pay,
    bill);
  - pay and bill overtime policy versions (the FK includes a constant
    side column);
  - calculation version;
  - priced_by and priced_at.

priced_timesheet_lines
  Source:
    - entry, assignment, shift, worker, relationship, facility;
    - discipline, classification, local date.
  Minutes: raw, priced, and the pay/bill regular and overtime split.
  Applied:
    - card, version, precedence;
    - currency, pay rate, bill rate;
    - rounding version, mode and increment;
    - overtime fraction per side.
  Also: amounts and calculation version.

Both tables refuse UPDATE, DELETE and TRUNCATE for every role.

======================================================================
12. PRICING ENGINE
======================================================================
internal.price_timesheet_revision(timesheet, expected_revision) is the
only implementation; public.price_timesheet (pricing.run) calls it. Steps:

  1. Lock the timesheet. Require locked status (CHM07) and the expected
     revision (CHM08).
  2. Return an existing record for this revision ('existing').
  3. Read the current approval snapshot.
  4. Per entry, in chronological order:
       - resolve the rate;
       - check the currency;
       - apply rounding;
       - apply overtime;
       - compute amounts.
  5. If there are issues:
       - write pricing_blocks;
       - audit pricing.failed;
       - notify once;
       - return 'blocked' with the issues.
  6. Otherwise:
       - insert the header and lines;
       - resolve the block;
       - audit pricing.created (and pricing.repriced_for_revision for a
         later revision).

No frontend pricing math exists. Pricing has no payroll, invoicing or
ledger side effects.

======================================================================
13. WORKED-MINUTE SOURCE
======================================================================
Raw minutes come only from the approval snapshot of the locked revision,
using worked_minutes per entry.

  - Each snapshot entry is cross-checked against the locked
    timesheet_entries row (same worked minutes and revision). Any
    divergence fails with CHM08.
  - Live attendance, scheduled hours and client input are never used.
  - Not-worked (0-minute) entries are not priced.

pgTAP check S asserts that priced raw minutes equal the approval total.

======================================================================
14. ROUNDING POLICY
======================================================================
Default: none.

  - Versioned agency policy: none, or nearest 5, 6, 10 or 15 minutes.
  - The policy in force on the work date applies.
  - Rounding is per timesheet entry, half up:
    round_half_up(raw / N) × N. For example, 455 at 10 minutes gives 460;
    453 at 15 minutes gives 450.
  - Both raw_minutes and priced_minutes are stored. Source minutes are
    never changed.
  - One rounding applies to both pay and bill (documented).

======================================================================
15. OVERTIME POLICY FOUNDATION
======================================================================
Implemented, but off unless configured.

  - Separate pay and bill policies.
  - A weekly threshold (60–10,080 minutes) and an exact multiplier
    fraction of at least 1×.
  - The week is the timesheet period. The policy in force on the period's
    first day applies.
  - Priced minutes accumulate in chronological order:
    regular = max(0, min(priced, threshold − counted)).
  - Documented as not legal advice. No jurisdictional rules are built in;
    daily overtime, tiers and regular-rate methods are deferred.

======================================================================
16. CALCULATION ARITHMETIC
======================================================================
Calculation version 1:

  round_half_up(a/b) = (2a + b) div (2b)
  amount = round_half_up(rate × (regular × den + overtime × num) / (60 × den))

Each line is rounded once to the minor unit; totals are sums of lines.

Tested examples:
  - 480 min × $42.50 = $340.00.
  - 480 min × $58.00 = $464.00.
  - 453 min × $42.50 = 1,925,250 / 60 = 32,087.5, rounded half up to
    $320.88.
  - 453 min × $58.00 = $437.90.
  - Totals: $660.88 pay, $901.90 bill.
  - Revision 2 with rounding to 15 minutes, pay overtime after 10 h at
    1.5×, and a new rate of $44.00 from day 2:
      pay $451.00 (45100 = 4400 × (120×2 + 330×3) / 120);
      bill $450.00.

======================================================================
17. PRICING REVISION MODEL
======================================================================
Pricing is unique per (timesheet, revision); repricing returns the
existing record. When a timesheet is reopened and locked again, the new
revision is priced as a new record:

  - the old record stays;
  - the UI marks it "Superseded by revision N";
  - the event is audited as pricing.repriced_for_revision.

Pricing an old revision fails with CHM08.

======================================================================
18. MISSING / AMBIGUOUS RATE HANDLING
======================================================================
Any of these blocks the whole timesheet:
  - RATE_NOT_CONFIGURED;
  - RATE_AMBIGUOUS;
  - RATE_CURRENCY_MISMATCH.

When blocked:
  - nothing is priced; there is no zero, no guess and no partial pricing;
  - pricing_blocks holds the issues per entry (date, discipline,
    classification, code) and counts attempts;
  - /pricing "Needs attention" lists the issues with a link to Rates;
  - "Price again" works once a rate exists, and the block is resolved,
    not deleted.

======================================================================
19. RELATIONSHIP-END S7 RESOLUTION
======================================================================
A trigger fires when agency_facility_relationships.status becomes ended,
on any path. It runs internal.release_facility_signoffs. For each
agency_approved timesheet with that relationship's entries still pending:

  - the timesheet is locked FOR UPDATE (after the relationship row, which
    the status change already holds);
  - those entries become not_required;
  - timesheet_history records facility_signoff_not_required, reason
    relationship_ended;
  - the change is audited as timesheet.signoff_not_required, with counts;
  - if nothing is left pending or disputed, the timesheet locks.

Concurrency and authority:
  - facility_decide_timesheet_entry now takes the relationship lock
    (FOR SHARE) before the timesheet lock;
  - it is refused once the relationship has ended (CHP12);
  - an agency answering an open dispute on an ended relationship makes
    the entry not_required, not pending.

Historical decisions remain. No manual reopen is needed. Tested in pgTAP,
integration and E2E.

32. KNOWN LIMITATIONS
======================================================================
  - Rates are hourly only. There is no flat, daily, per diem, mileage,
    bonus, holiday, call-back or differential stacking.
  - One rounding policy for both pay and bill, per entry only.
  - Overtime is weekly-threshold only, with one tier.
  - Currencies with non-2-digit minor units are not seeded. The model
    supports the digit count; the UI parser assumes 2.
  - No CSV rate import.
  - No worker pay visibility.
  - No facility bill visibility.
  - A rate cannot be ended early without a new version or a gap: active
    terms are frozen by design.
  - Entries with 0 worked minutes ("not worked") are not priced.
