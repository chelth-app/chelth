4. KPI AREA (region "Timesheets by status"; each card is a real status filter)
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Pending Submission = open. Supporting "Not yet submitted". Footer: number
  with shifts to resolve.
- Needs Review = submitted. Supporting "Submitted for review". Footer:
  number with shifts to resolve.
- Approved = locked, meaning agency-approved with facility sign-off
  complete. Footer: number awaiting facility (agency_approved).
- Needs Correction = rejected (product label "Returned"). Footer: facility
  discrepancies in the week.

Counts are for the chosen week, from one read. The active card carries
aria-current. There are no sparklines, and no paid or processed figures.


5. FILTERS (form "Filter timesheets"; Facilities control geometry)
------------------------------------------------------------------
- Week starting (period) and Status are the existing list filters, sent to
  list_agency_timesheets.
- Worker, Facility and Search narrow only the week already loaded. Options
  come from the loaded rows; no new query parameter reaches the database.
- "Show" submits the form. "Clear filters" appears when any filter is set.


6. TIMESHEET TABLE ("Current Timesheets"; region "Agency timesheets table")
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
- REF_TABLE with 42 px rows.
- Columns:
  - Worker: avatar, with the name linking to the record;
  - Facility: the facilities worked that week;
  - Week: compact range, with the full week in the title tooltip;
  - Shifts;
  - Worked Hours;
  - Source: "Attendance";
  - Status: chip plus a flags line (Revision n · n shifts to resolve ·
    Discrepancy · Awaiting facility);
  - ⋯ "Timesheet Details" drawer trigger.
- The selected row gets the teal inset rule and a mint fill.

- No editing is implied anywhere; reopening stays the existing "Reopen as a
  new revision" form.


10. TIMESHEET DRAWER ("Timesheet Details"; canonical drawer)
------------------------------------------------------------
- Identity: 84 px avatar, worker, status chip, "Weekly timesheet".
- Meta rows: facilities; week · shifts.
- Tabs:
  - Overview:
    - Time Details: week, shifts, break deduction, worked hours, source
      "Attendance (derived)", adjustments (approved corrections), approval
      status, revision.
    - Attendance Verification: success tile, or the shifts to resolve with
      reasons.
    - Facility Sign-off (when approved).
    - Audit History: the 3 latest events, plus a "Full history" link.
  - Shifts: each entry with clock-in/out, worked time and an attendance
    record link.
  - History: full timeline.
- Actions:
  - Primary: "Review and approve" (approvers, submitted timesheets) opens
    the record's Approval section. Otherwise "Open timesheet record".
  - Secondary: "View Attendance" (the attendance window for that week) and
    "View Shift" (the exact shift when the week has one), or "View Shifts"
    (the week in Shifts).
- Capability-gated: attendance.view and shift.view.

16. INTENTIONAL PRODUCT / DATA DIFFERENCES
------------------------------------------
- Timesheets are WEEKLY per worker, with entries per shift. The reference
  rows are per shift, so the per-row Shift Date / Check In / Check Out /
  Break become Week / Shifts / Worked Hours. Per-shift times are in the
  drawer's Shifts tab and on the record.
- Source: every Chelth timesheet is derived from attendance; the
  Manual / Adjusted sources don't exist. Approved corrections appear as
  "Adjustments".
- Not offered: Export (no export capability exists), worker role and photo,
  "View All Activity" (no activity route), and in-drawer Approve / Request
  Correction (approval stays on the record with the existing forms;
  "Request Correction" = the existing "Return to worker").
- Recent Timesheet Activity is built from the history of the timesheets
  listed in the current view (the first 25), the 6 most recent events.
- Drawer details (entries and history) are read for the first 25 listed
  timesheets. Further rows show list facts and link to the record.
- KPI "Approved" counts locked timesheets only; the agency-approved ones
  waiting for facility sign-off are its footer.
- Drawer docking: the reference canvas shows the drawer docked. The locked
  drawer docks at 1536 px and wider, so screenshot 04 (1512) shows the
  modal and screenshots 10/11 (1536) show the docked layout.
- At docked 1536 width:
  - KPI labels wrap to two lines, because the locked card type is wider
    than the reference cards.
  - The "awaiting facility" footer clips slightly; the copy was shortened
    to reduce this.
  - The filter row wraps to two lines.
  These follow from the locked geometry, so they were not changed.
- QA data: 2 timesheets (Tia submitted, 11 h 30 min; Cy open with a shift
  to resolve) and 1 activity event.
