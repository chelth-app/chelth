Preserve the exact approved visual language:

- no additional gradients beyond what is represented in the reference

Use the canonical approved Chelth Shift Mark / wordmark from public/brand/chelth/.

5. SLIDESHOW
The hero image area is a seamless slideshow.

NON-NEGOTIABLE:
- NO image border
- NO framed carousel container
- NO visible rectangular image edge
- NO image card shadow
- NO rounded slideshow box
- imagery must blend directly into the hero background using edge masks / fades

Slide order:
01 — Supervisor showing Black nurse the shift dashboard
02 — Coverage / staffing activity
03 — Nurse scheduling on computer
04 — Operational dashboard

Interaction:
- autoplay approximately every 6.5 seconds
- soft crossfade
- slight 1.025 → 1 scale transition
- pause on hover/focus
- resume after manual interaction
- dots for direct navigation
- keyboard left/right navigation
- pointer/touch swipe where practical
- respect prefers-reduced-motion

7. EXPLAINER RAIL
The explainer is intentionally SMALL and secondary.

Do not enlarge it.

It should:
- remain compact
- sit below the main image subject area
- avoid covering important people/UI
- update with each slide
- use subtle white/translucent surface treatment
- not compete visually with the hero headline or side cards

Slide 01:
Coordinate the shift with clarity.
Give supervisors and healthcare professionals one clear view of schedules, staffing needs and what requires attention.

Slide 02:
See what still needs coverage.
Keep open shifts, staffing requests and outstanding confirmations visible before they become urgent.

Slide 03:
Keep your workforce ready.
Bring availability, credential readiness and upcoming assignments together around the work ahead.

Slide 04:
Run the operation from one clear view.
Bring shifts, staffing requests, workforce readiness, credentials and facility activity together in one operational view.
