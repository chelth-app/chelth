## Brand rules

- The approved Chelth logo is **The Shift Mark**.
- Do **not** redraw, regenerate, approximate, substitute, stretch, crop into, or recolor the mark.
- Do **not** manually typeset “Chelth” beside the symbol when an approved lockup exists.
- Do **not** create a new icon, monogram, or alternate C-mark.
- Use the exact assets in `/public/brand/chelth/`.
